Tenet

CodeLabelDefinition
EverythingIsAResourceEverything is a resourceEvery data source and computing service is a resource, whatever its size, including the devices an organization lets reach its resources.
CommunicationIsSecuredAnywhereCommunication is secured anywhereEvery communication is protected for confidentiality, integrity and the authentication of its source, and a network location alone grants no trust.
AccessIsPerSessionAccess is per sessionAccess to one resource is granted for one session with the least privilege the task needs, after the requester is evaluated; a grant for one resource carries to no other.
PolicyIsDynamicPolicy is dynamicAccess is decided by rules over the observable state of the identity, the application and the requesting asset, and over behavior and the environment, stricter as the resource is more sensitive.
PostureIsMonitoredPosture is monitoredThe organization measures the integrity and security posture of every asset it owns or is associated with, and trusts no asset for what it is.
EnforcementIsDynamicAuthentication and authorization are dynamic and enforcedAuthentication and authorization are repeated on the events the policy names and strictly enforced before access, as a constant cycle of access, assessment and adaptation.
StateIsCollectedState is collectedThe organization collects as much as it can about the state of its assets, network and communications and uses it to improve the policy and its enforcement.

CodeLabelDefinition
IdentityIdentityWho or what asks for access: enterprise-managed identities, strong authentication that resists phishing, and the identity systems that hold them.

PreparerPreparerThe actor that made the artifact of the act: a document, a change, a test or a plan. Any kind of actor can prepare.
AssistantAssistantAn agent or a system that operated a tool during a step of the act, under a directive and a tasking.
ExecutorExecutorThe person, or the validated mechanism, that carried the step out.
ReviewerReviewerA person who read the artifact and signed that review; for a regulated act, not the person who prepared it.

ActorKind

CodeLabelDefinition
PersonPersonA human being, who can be accountable and can sign.
SystemSystemA deterministic mechanism that a person validated: the engine, a typed verb, a pipeline.
AgentAgentAn AI agent: a model run under instructions with tools. It is instructed, not accountable, and what it makes is a draft until a person or a validated mechanism clears it.

TaskingLevel

CodeLabelDefinition
T0SuggestT0, suggestThe agent only suggests; nothing it makes enters a record.
T1PrepareT1, prepareThe agent prepares drafts in a branch or a sandbox; a person reviews them before they enter a record.
T2AssistExecutionT2, assist executionThe agent assists a step whose plan hash a person approved for each step; the engine carries it out.
T3AssistReversibleT3, assist reversible stepsThe agent assists reversible, low risk steps under a standing directive and a current qualification; the owner of the directive stays the initiator and the engine stays the executor.

Code lists

EvidenceGrade

CodeLabelDefinition
E0E0 noneNo evidence: a requirement with no record of any kind.
E1E1 statedA person or an agent says the requirement is met; nothing ran.
E2E2 reviewedA review is recorded against the requirement, by a person or by an agent (said to be an agent's).
E3E3 testedAn automated run proved the requirement and its record exists.
E4E4 attestedThe run is kept on the quality branch, chained, signed and linked to its commit, so that a third party can verify it.
E5E5 observedA probe of the system in operation reports the requirement met: operational truth.

TestOutcome

CodeLabelDefinition
PasspassThe case ran and its assertions held.
FailfailThe case ran and an assertion did not hold.
SkipskipA runner skipped the case on purpose, with its reason.
TodotodoThe case is declared and not yet written, with its reason.
CancelledcancelledThe case started and did not end before its process did.
NotRunnot runA planned case, check or requirement that never started, with the reason: it is never counted as passed.

FreshnessState

CodeLabelDefinition
FreshfreshThe proof is within its cadence and nothing in its chain changed after its run.
DuedueThe proof is within a tenth of its cadence of its end.
StalestaleThe proof is past its cadence or a file of its chain changed after its run: it opens a nonconformance and does not count at its grade.

NonconformanceState

CodeLabelDefinition
NonconformanceOpenopenThe system opened it and no CAPA of it is closed.
NonconformanceClosedclosedIts CAPA is closed: verified by a named test and approved by a named person.

CapaState

CodeLabelDefinition
CapaOpenopenThe corrective action is stated and its named test has not passed after the corrective change.
CapaVerifiedverifiedThe named test passed in a run after the corrective change; a person has not yet approved the closure.
CapaClosedclosedA named person approved the closure of a verified CAPA, recorded as a Decision.

QualityTier

CodeLabelDefinition
Tier0Tier 0Evidence exists: one canonical record per run, kept immutable and chained on the quality branch.
Tier1Tier 1The system reports itself: grades, review debt, freshness, nonconformance, CAPA and objectives.
Tier2Tier 2People see and prove: the console, attested samples, pre-registered plans, mutation runs, the public page and the assessor pack.

Audience

CodeLabelDefinition
PublicpublicAnyone: figures, grades and failures with their requirement and reason, and nothing that names a person, customer, host or private repository.
AssessorassessorA person who verifies the evidence: the full records, with secrets and personal data redacted only.

# Restore drill

## When to use

Prove that a backup of a transactional store restores to the same digests, in a disposable workshop, and keep the evidence for the audit.

## How to run it

You orchestrate: every effect is a verb of the pinned packages, called by its id as `bun run runbooks verb <id>` and never by a command built from the text of a step. A `judge` step only proposes: record what it proposes and apply nothing from it. At an `ask` or a `gate` stop and hand back: a person answers, and you never approve your own work.

## Inputs

- `order` (text, required)

## Verbs

- `store.backup` (@intellectual-frontiers/data): Copy a store into a backup store; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-acts-human-reviews
- `store.checkpoint` (@intellectual-frontiers/data): Make a restore point of a store before it is changed; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-acts-human-reviews
- `store.digests` (@intellectual-frontiers/crypto): Read the digests of the objects of two stores and say whether they are the same; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-autonomous
- `store.restore` (@intellectual-frontiers/data): Restore a backup into a store; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-autonomous
- `work.claim` (@intellectual-frontiers/planning): Claim a work order for a person or the agent that acts for them; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-acts-human-reviews
- `work.record` (@intellectual-frontiers/planning): Record evidence on a work order; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-proposes
- `workshop.attach` (@intellectual-frontiers/anyfs): Attach a store to the workshop by the capabilities it needs; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-autonomous
- `workshop.detach` (@intellectual-frontiers/anyfs): Detach a store from the workshop; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-autonomous
- `workshop.dispose` (@intellectual-frontiers/anyfs): Dispose of the workshop; it must be disposable, which is computed from what is attached; the strictest autonomy of its steps is agent-autonomous

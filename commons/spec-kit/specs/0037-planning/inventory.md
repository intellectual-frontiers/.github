# Inventory: where an issue-like record exists or is read

**For:** 0037-planning (Planning). **Read:** the two repositories on 2026-11-21: this one, and the company's private monorepo, read only (it is named `the monorepo` here and its paths and specification numbers are not written in this public repository). Each entry says what is stored, its id, its states, its fields, who writes and who reads it, and how it maps onto the model of 0037-planning: **as is** (the model holds it with a field or a value it already has), **with a field** (the model holds it once a field of the core names it) or **not covered** (the model cannot hold it without a type it does not have). Nothing below is a plan to change the record: Planning reads each of them and writes back only in the form of the place (FR-015).

The scope of the first version is the existing issue uses (entries 1 to 14). The entries of 15 to 22 are records that are findings, notes or evidence and not issues: they are listed so that the place where each would attach later is known, and none is modeled now (see the later phase of `plan.md`).

## C. Entries that were searched and are not issue records

- `integrations-github` reads the posture and repositories of an organization read-only: it holds no issue.
- `commons/telemetry` and the journal of `evidence-capture` are a journal of runs: not issues.
- The words `ticket` and `backlog` name no record in either repository (no source file or specification names one).
- The monorepo keeps an older issues capability and an older publication console under a folder named `retired`: they are not read as a source here.

## D. What the inventory justifies and what it does not

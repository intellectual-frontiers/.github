# Feature Specification: Boundaries

**Spec ID:** 0045-boundaries
**Status:** Draft
**Owner:** if
**Domain:** commons
**Part of:** 0043-asset-properties
**Governs:** estate 0010-estate-boundaries

**Input:** Every regime that asks an organization to show it is in control starts by asking what the organization answers for. A federal system answers for its **authorization boundary**; a defense contractor for its **CUI environment** and the assets in the categories of the CMMC rule; a covered entity for its **ePHI environment** and, when it is one legal entity doing covered and other work, for its **health care components**; a merchant for its **cardholder data environment**; a service organization for the **system** its report describes; a controller for the personal data and processing activities it answers for, in the regime that governs them; a provider of managed services for each client; a network for its **zones**; a threat model for its **trust boundaries**; a penetration test for the **scope of its engagement**. Each draws a line around the things it is accountable for, calls what is inside by names the regime defines, names what crosses the line, and says what it relies on from others. The estate (`estate`) holds assets, links, sightings and readings, but nothing in it says **who answers for an asset and under which rule**. This specification adds that: the **boundary** as a typed, versioned, signed declaration of an accountability scope of a stated kind, the **disposition** of every asset in every boundary (in scope in a named role, inherited, external, out of scope with a justification, or not yet decided), the **interconnections** that cross a boundary and the agreements that allow them, the **inheritance** of what other parties provide, the posture of a boundary as a projection of the claim table (0043-asset-properties), the projections that regimes ask for (the OSCAL system security plan, the CMMC system security plan, the SOC 2 system description, the HIPAA risk analysis scope, the records of processing, the PCI scope), the suggestion of the smallest boundary that holds what a data class touches, and the **interlock** by which an active tool refuses a target outside the approved boundary. A **boundary** is not a **realm** (0044-asset-connections): a realm is where assets are found, a boundary is where they are accountable. The model is an algebra in `estate` (estate 0010-estate-boundaries): pure, total functions over typed values, with laws that property tests prove. The definitions in the words of each regime, with their sources and what was not read, are in `definitions.md`; the reuse audit is `reuse-audit.md`. **Every requirement that asks for code is Deferred: this specification and the ones it governs hold no code.**

## The boundary

- **FR-001**: A boundary MUST be a typed value `Boundary<K>` of a kind `K` (FR-002): `{ id, kind, name, owner, roles, authority, categorization, include, exclude, overrides, parents, constraints, interconnections, inheritances, activities, version }`: its id (lower case words joined by hyphens, made by a smart constructor), its kind, a name, the owner (FR-005), the holders of the other roles of the kind, the authority (FR-008), the categorization (FR-006), the selectors that say what it takes in and what it leaves out (FR-020), the overrides a person made (FR-022), the boundaries it is a part of (FR-028), the constraints between boundaries (FR-029), the interconnections that cross it (FR-040), the inheritances it relies on (FR-050), the processing activities where its kind has them (FR-032) and the version. The type of a boundary MUST depend on its kind, so that a role, a disposition or a requirement of one kind does not type-check in another. Relies on audit rows: A1, A2.
- **FR-002**: The kinds of boundary MUST be one closed table that only grows, each with its authority, the roles of its people, the roles an asset can have inside it (FR-024) and the agreements that cross it (FR-043):
  - `authorization`: the authorization boundary of an information system (FISMA, FedRAMP);
  - `cui-environment`: the assessment scope of an organization that handles controlled unclassified information (CMMC, NIST SP 800-171);
  - `ephi-environment`: the electronic protected health information a covered entity or a business associate answers for (HIPAA), with the health care component of a hybrid entity as a part of its organization;
  - `cardholder-data-environment` (PCI DSS);
  - `soc2-system`: the system a service organization's report describes, with its subservice organizations;
  - `privacy`: the personal data and the processing activities one controller or business answers for under one regime (FR-003);
  - `organizational`: a company, a legal entity, a tenant, a client of a provider of managed services, or a part of one;
  - `network-zone`: an enclave, a segment or a zone, with its managed interfaces;
  - `trust`: a trust boundary of a threat model;
  - `engagement-scope`: the targets, actions and window that a signed engagement allows.
  A kind MUST NOT be added without its definition and source in `definitions.md` and a row of its standards in the table of this specification. Relies on audit rows: A1, A3.
- **FR-003**: A boundary of the kind `privacy` MUST name its **regime** from a closed table that only grows: `gdpr`, `uk-gdpr`, `ccpa-cpra`, `lgpd`, `pipeda`, `pdpa-sg` and `dpdp`. Each regime MUST carry as data its **role words** (the controller and the processor of the GDPR and the UK GDPR; the business, the service provider, the contractor and the third party of the CCPA and the CPRA; the controlador and the operador of the LGPD; the organization of PIPEDA; the organisation and the data intermediary of the PDPA; the Data Fiduciary, the Data Processor and the Significant Data Fiduciary of the DPDP Act), its **legal bases** or the grounds it allows for processing (a closed table of the regime), its **agreement kinds** (FR-043), its **transfer mechanisms** (FR-047) and the form of its record of processing (FR-075). A regime whose text has not been read (`definitions.md`, UNVERIFIED) MUST be marked `unread` in the table, and a boundary of an `unread` regime MUST NOT be approved (FR-067) until its text is read and the mark is removed by a person's change. A boundary MUST be able to carry more than one regime only as several boundaries of the same assets, never as a merged one. Relies on audit rows: A3, A4.
- **FR-004**: A boundary MUST be a document of the fleet repository, written as typed code (`boundaries/<id>.ts`) with a journal (`boundaries/<id>.journal.json`, FR-101), decoded on read by the same decoders as every other document of the fleet (estate 0005-estate-fleet-documents), and it MUST hold no secret and no token. The real names of the organization, its people and its systems MUST live only in the private fleet repository; the commons, this specification and its tests use synthetic names (`synthetic-<kind>-NN`). Relies on audit rows: A2, A6.
- **FR-005**: Each kind MUST name the **roles of its people** as a closed table (for the kind `authorization`: the authorizing official, the system owner, the information system security officer and the common control provider; for `cui-environment`: the senior official of the organization seeking assessment and the person who answers for the system security plan; for `ephi-environment`: the covered entity's responsible officer and the business associates' contacts; for `privacy`: the controller's contact and the data protection officer where the regime requires one; for `engagement-scope`: the client's approver and the lead of the testers; and the equivalent for the others), and every role MUST be held by a named principal of the fleet's private names. The **owner** of a boundary MUST be the holder of the role that approves its changes (FR-067). One principal MAY hold several roles; where a kind's table says two roles are to be held apart, holding both MUST be a finding of kind `roles-not-separate` that a person accepts for a time and a reason in the typed accepted-finding file, and an agent MUST NOT write it (FR-068). Relies on audit rows: A1, F1.
- **FR-006**: A boundary MUST carry a **categorization** as typed data: for each of the three security objectives (confidentiality, integrity, availability) an impact of `low`, `moderate` or `high`, as FIPS 199 states, together with the information types and data classes the boundary holds (FR-007) and the rationale for each. The categorization of a boundary MUST be the join (the high water mark) over its information types, per objective, with `low < moderate < high`, and a type that states no impact for an objective MUST be refused. The join MUST be a function of the members: adding an asset that holds an information type MUST NOT lower any objective. A kind that does not use FIPS 199 (a boundary of the kind `engagement-scope`, for one) MAY leave the categorization empty and MUST say so. Relies on audit rows: A1, B1.
- **FR-007**: A **data class** MUST be one of a closed table that only grows and that names, for each, the regime or authority that defines it: `fci` (federal contract information), `cui` (with its category from the registry the organization uses), `phi` and `ephi`, `personal-data`, `special-category-data` (and the regime's own words for it), `cardholder-data` and `sensitive-authentication-data`, `security-protection-data`, `confidential`, `internal` and `public`. A data class MUST be a property of an asset's reading (0043-asset-properties) or an attribute that a person states, and the **location** of an asset (a jurisdiction, FR-047) MUST be an attribute of the asset or of its realm. Relies on audit rows: B1, B2.
- **FR-008**: The **authority** of a boundary MUST be typed data: the framework and its version (a catalog of `grc`), the requirements it asks for by their control ids (as the catalog states them: for SP 800-53 and SP 800-171, the ids of the catalogs in this repository), and the party that assesses it (an authorizing official, a certification body, a service auditor, a regulator, a client). The requirements of a kind that no catalog holds MUST be named by their source and number and MUST be marked `uncatalogued`. Relies on audit rows: A3, B3.
- **FR-009**: A boundary and a realm (0044-asset-connections) MUST be different types: a **realm** says where assets are found and what a connection may read, and a **boundary** says who answers for an asset and under which rule. An asset belongs to one realm or more (the sources that saw it) and to as many boundaries as declare it. A boundary MUST NOT name a realm as its membership by that alone: a selector of the realm (FR-020) is one way to select assets. Relies on audit rows: A1, A5.
- **FR-010**: The **overlap** of two boundaries MUST be computed, never declared: for two boundaries, the assets in scope of both, in scope of one and not the other, and the roles they have in each. The overlap of every pair of boundaries of one fleet MUST be one typed document that every surface prints. Relies on audit rows: A1.
- **FR-011**: A boundary MUST be refused on read when: its kind and its roles do not agree, a role has no holder, a selector does not decode, an override names an asset that is not in the estate, a part-of cycle exists (FR-028), a constraint names a boundary that is not in the fleet, or an interconnection names an end that is neither a boundary of the fleet nor an external system (FR-031); each refusal MUST name the document, the field and the reason (estate 0005-estate-fleet-documents). Relies on audit rows: A2.
- **FR-012**: The **names** a boundary shows (its name, the names of its roles, the names of its external systems) MUST be one table in the fleet's private names, and every surface MUST print the identifier where the name is not available, so that no real name is needed to read, test or exchange a boundary in a public place. Relies on audit rows: A6.

## Selectors and membership

- **FR-020**: A **selector** MUST be a typed expression of a closed grammar and not a free text query: the atoms `class` (a kind of asset of 0007-estate-kinds), `tag`, `id`, `realm`, `segment`, `owner` (a party of `tenancy`), `data-class` (FR-007), `location` (a jurisdiction), `role-of` (the role an asset has in another boundary) and `source` (a source that saw it); and the combinators `and`, `or` and `not`. The empty `and` MUST select every asset and the empty `or` none. Relies on audit rows: A1, B4.
- **FR-021**: `select(selector, estate)` MUST be a pure, total, deterministic function giving the set of ids of the assets of the estate that the selector takes in, whatever the order of the assets, and the combinators MUST obey the laws of a Boolean algebra of sets: commutativity, associativity, idempotence, absorption, the identities, the complement and De Morgan's laws (estate 0010-estate-boundaries FR-011). Relies on audit rows: A1.
- **FR-022**: The **members** of a boundary MUST be `select(include) \ select(exclude)`, with **overrides** applied after: an override puts one named asset in or out, always with the principal who made it, the instant and a reason, and an override MUST beat a selector. The members MUST be a pure function of the boundary and the estate, MUST NOT depend on the order of the selectors, and MUST be stable (the same boundary and estate give the same ordered list). Relies on audit rows: A1.
- **FR-023**: A **disposition** MUST be a total, single-valued function from the pair of a boundary and an asset to one value of the closed sum `InScope(role)`, `Inherited(inheritance)`, `External(system)`, `OutOfScope(justification)` and `Undecided`, where the role belongs to the kind of the boundary (FR-024) and `Undecided` is the value of every asset that nothing has decided. The disposition of an asset MUST be one value for one boundary at one instant, never two. Relies on audit rows: A1.
- **FR-024**: The **roles of an asset inside a boundary** MUST be one closed table per kind, that only grows:
  - `authorization`: `component`, `subsystem-member`, `security-function`;
  - `cui-environment`: `cui-asset`, `security-protection-asset`, `contractor-risk-managed-asset` and `specialized-asset` (the four in-scope categories of the CMMC rule), each with its assessment treatment as data;
  - `ephi-environment`: `ephi-system` (creates, receives, maintains or transmits ePHI), `business-associate-system` and `supporting-system`;
  - `cardholder-data-environment`: `cde-system`, `connected-to-system` and `security-impacting-system`;
  - `soc2-system`: `infrastructure`, `software`, `people`, `procedures` and `data` (the components a service organization's description names) and `subservice-system`;
  - `privacy`: `processing-system`, `processor-system` and `recipient-system`;
  - `organizational`: `owned`, `managed-for-client` and `leased`;
  - `network-zone`: `member` and `managed-interface`;
  - `trust`: `process`, `store`, `external-entity` and `data-flow-end`;
  - `engagement-scope`: `target`, `excluded-target` and `support-system`.
  Each role MUST name the requirements it brings (FR-061). A role that a kind's table does not list MUST NOT type-check for that kind. Relies on audit rows: A1, A3.
- **FR-025**: The disposition `OutOfScope` MUST carry a justification (typed: the rule it rests on, the evidence, the principal who made it, the instant and an expiry), MUST be a person's act that an agent cannot make, and MUST be refused for an asset that meets an in-scope rule of the kind (FR-026): an asset in an in-scope category MUST NOT be out of scope (as the CMMC rule says), and an `OutOfScope` disposition that a later reading contradicts (for example an asset that now holds a data class that makes it in scope, or one that now connects to an in-scope asset without separation) MUST become the finding `out-of-scope-contradicted` that names the reading. Out-of-scope is a decision with evidence and MUST be shown as one: the register of out-of-scope assets and their justifications is a view (FR-072) and is never the complement of the in-scope list. Relies on audit rows: A1, A3.
- **FR-026**: Each kind MUST carry **classification rules** as data: given an asset, its attributes, the data classes of its readings and its links, a rule proposes a role (for the kind `cui-environment`: an asset that processes, stores or transmits CUI proposes `cui-asset`; one that provides security functions, `security-protection-asset`; one that can but is not meant to handle CUI because of the organization's policy, `contractor-risk-managed-asset`; one that cannot be fully secured, `specialized-asset`; one that none of these fits and that is separated, `OutOfScope`). A rule MUST only **propose**: a proposed disposition MUST be shown with its rule and its evidence and MUST become a disposition only by a person's approval (FR-067); and the rules of a kind MUST be data of the library, versioned, and not code of a product. Relies on audit rows: A1, A3.
- **FR-027**: The **coverage** of a boundary MUST be the share of the assets that should have a disposition in it (every asset that any selector, any rule or any link of the kind places in its neighbourhood) that have one other than `Undecided`; an asset with no decision MUST be a finding of kind `no-disposition` that names the asset and the boundary, and the register of assets that belong to no boundary of a kind the fleet has declared MUST be one typed view (`unscoped`) that every surface prints. A fleet with no boundary at all MUST show every asset as unscoped, never as in order. Relies on audit rows: A1, E1.
- **FR-028**: A boundary MAY be a **part of** another of the same kind (a subsystem of a system, a health care component of an ephi environment, an enclave of a zone), and the members of the part MUST be a subset of the members of the whole in the roles the kind says (for the kind `authorization`, every asset in scope of a subsystem is in scope of its system); a part whose asset is not in its whole MUST be the finding `part-outside-whole`; and a cycle of parts MUST be refused. Relies on audit rows: A1.
- **FR-029**: A **constraint** MUST be a typed relation between two boundaries or between a boundary and an attribute, from a closed table: `within(a, b)` (the members of `a` are members of `b`), `disjoint(a, b)`, `equals(a, b)` and `residency(a, jurisdictions)` (the assets of `a` have a location among the jurisdictions). A constraint that does not hold MUST be a finding of kind `constraint-broken` that names the assets that break it; constraints are checked, never assumed. Relies on audit rows: A1, A4.
- **FR-030**: A **population** (0044-asset-connections FR-036) MUST have one disposition for the population and for its members as a group, and a member MAY have its own only by an override (FR-022); a member that appears while a population is in scope MUST take the population's disposition, so that ephemeral members never fall into `Undecided` for a boundary their population is in. Relies on audit rows: A1.
- **FR-031**: A system that is not in the fleet but that a boundary relies on or exchanges information with (a cloud provider's authorized service, a subservice organization, a business associate, a processor, an external service provider) MUST be an **external system**: `{ id, name, party, kind, authorization }`, where the authorization is a reference to what the party holds (a FedRAMP authorization, a SOC 2 report and its period, a certification, a business associate agreement, a contract) with its dates, made by a smart constructor. An external system MUST be the disposition `External` of the assets of the fleet that stand for it, or the end of an interconnection or of an inheritance, and MUST never be in the fleet's register as an asset the fleet manages. Relies on audit rows: A1, C3.
- **FR-032**: A boundary of the kind `privacy` MUST carry **processing activities** `{ id, purpose, basis, dataSubjects, dataClasses, systems, recipients, transfers, retention, measures }`: the purpose in words of the regime, the legal basis from the regime's table (FR-003), the categories of people and of data, the assets that do the processing (selectors), the recipients (external systems or interconnections), the transfers (FR-047), how long the data is kept and the measures; and the assets of an activity MUST be in scope of the boundary in a role of the kind. Relies on audit rows: A4, B2.

## Interconnections and flows

- **FR-040**: An **interconnection** MUST be a typed value `{ id, from, to, direction, dataClasses, protocols, agreement, interface, approvedBy, approvedAt, reviewedAt, reviewEvery, expires }`: its two ends (a boundary of the fleet or an external system, FR-031), the direction (`one-way-out`, `one-way-in` or `two-way`), the data classes it carries (FR-007), the protocols, the agreement (FR-043), the managed interface it goes through (an asset in the role `managed-interface`, FR-045), and its approval, its review and its expiry. An interconnection MUST be written once in the document of each boundary that it crosses and the two copies MUST agree (FR-011). Relies on audit rows: A1, A3.
- **FR-041**: A link of the estate (estate 0007-estate-kinds FR-003) or a flow that a source observed (NetFlow, a flow log, a resource's dependency, a firewall rule) **crosses** a boundary when one end is in scope in a role and the other is not (it is external, out of scope, undecided or in scope of a boundary of another kind only), and `crossings(boundary, estate)` MUST be a pure function giving every crossing with the link, the ends and the evidence. A crossing MUST be symmetric in its ends: the crossing from `a` to `b` of a boundary is the crossing from `b` to `a`. Relies on audit rows: A1, D1.
- **FR-042**: Every crossing MUST be covered by exactly one approved interconnection of the boundary, in force and not expired; a crossing with none MUST be the finding `unapproved-crossing` that names the link, the ends and the evidence, and an interconnection that no crossing uses MUST be the finding `interconnection-unused`; adding an approved interconnection MUST NOT create a finding, and removing a crossing MUST NOT create one (the findings are monotone in what is approved and what is seen). Relies on audit rows: A1, D1.
- **FR-043**: The **agreements** that allow a crossing MUST be one closed table that only grows, each naming the kinds of boundary it serves: `interconnection-security-agreement`, `information-exchange-agreement`, `memorandum-of-understanding` and `service-level-agreement` (the exchanges of SP 800-53 CA-3), `esp-agreement` (an external service provider of the CMMC rule), `business-associate-agreement` (HIPAA), `data-processing-agreement` (the contract that binds a processor, GDPR Article 28 and the equivalents of the other regimes), `transfer-mechanism` (FR-047), `service-provider-contract` (the CCPA and the CPRA), `subservice-agreement` (SOC 2) and `engagement-agreement` (the signed engagement of `tenancy`). An agreement MUST be a reference to a document that a person holds (never the text of a contract), with its dates. Relies on audit rows: A3, C2.
- **FR-044**: An interconnection MUST be **reviewed** on the interval it states (SP 800-53 CA-3(c)); an interconnection past its review date MUST be the finding `interconnection-review-overdue` and one past its expiry MUST be treated as no interconnection (FR-042). The review MUST be a person's act. Relies on audit rows: A3.
- **FR-045**: Where a kind requires that a crossing passes through a **managed interface** (the kinds `authorization`, `cui-environment`, `cardholder-data-environment` and `network-zone`, as SP 800-53 SC-7 and SP 800-171 03.13.01 say: external systems are reached only through boundary protection devices, and public-facing components sit on separated subnetworks), every crossing MUST name the asset that carries it in the role `managed-interface`, and a crossing with none MUST be the finding `crossing-without-managed-interface`. Relies on audit rows: A3, D1.
- **FR-046**: Where a source observes the data a flow carries (a labelled flow, a resource's tags, a proxy log), a flow that carries a data class the interconnection does not list MUST be the finding `flow-outside-agreement` that names the class, the flow and the interconnection; where no source says what a flow carries, the data class of the flow MUST be `unknown` and the finding MUST say that it cannot be judged, never that it is clean. Relies on audit rows: A1, D1.
- **FR-047**: A boundary of the kind `privacy` MUST know the **location** of its assets (a jurisdiction by its ISO 3166 code, or a named region of a provider mapped to one by a table of the fleet) and MUST treat a crossing from a jurisdiction to another as a **transfer**: the interconnection MUST name a transfer mechanism from the regime's closed table (for the GDPR: an adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or binding corporate rules, or a derogation of Article 49; for the other regimes, the mechanism the regime names), and a transfer with none MUST be the finding `transfer-without-mechanism`. A table of adequacy decisions MUST be data with its date and its source, and the model MUST NOT decide adequacy from its own memory (`definitions.md`, UNVERIFIED). Relies on audit rows: A4, B2.
- **FR-048**: The **data-flow diagram** of a boundary MUST be a typed value derived from the members, the links, the interconnections and the managed interfaces (nodes with their roles and data classes, edges with their direction, agreement and interface), and a renderer MUST only draw it (a typed diagram value that the diagram renderers of the repository draw as a figure); it MUST NOT hold a fact the model does not. Relies on audit rows: A1, G1.
- **FR-049**: A link between members of a boundary that are in different parts (FR-028) or different zones MUST be an **internal connection** and MUST be authorized, documented with its interface and the nature of its data, reviewed and ended on conditions as SP 800-53 CA-9 says, with the same agreements and findings as a crossing (`internal-connection-unapproved`). Relies on audit rows: A1, A3.

## Inheritance and what others provide

- **FR-050**: An **inheritance** MUST be a typed value `{ id, from, kind, controls, responsibility, evidence, validFrom, validTo }`: the provider (a boundary of the fleet or an external system), the kind (`leveraged-authorization`, `common-control`, `shared-responsibility` or `subservice-controls`), the controls it covers by id, the responsibility for each (`provider`, `customer` or `shared`), the evidence (a reference to the provider's package, report or certificate, FR-031) and its dates. Relies on audit rows: A1, C3.
- **FR-051**: The disposition `Inherited` MUST name an inheritance whose controls cover the requirements of the role (FR-061); a requirement that an inheritance covers MUST NOT also be shown as the boundary's own, and a requirement covered by none MUST be the boundary's own. The **responsibility matrix** of a boundary (each requirement, who answers for it, and the evidence) MUST be a view (FR-077). Relies on audit rows: A1, C3.
- **FR-052**: An inheritance MUST be **no weaker than what it supports**: the categorization of the provider (a boundary of the fleet, or the impact level its authorization states) MUST be at least the categorization of the inheriting boundary for each objective, and a provider below it MUST be the finding `inheritance-too-weak` that names the objective. Relies on audit rows: B1, C3.
- **FR-053**: The evidence of an inheritance MUST carry its dates (a SOC 2 report's period, an authorization's date and its review, a certificate's expiry), and an inheritance whose evidence is past its dates MUST be the finding `inheritance-evidence-expired`, with the controls that depend on it shown as having no evidence of their own. Relies on audit rows: C3, A3.
- **FR-054**: For the kind `cui-environment`, an external system that processes, stores or transmits CUI or security protection data MUST be handled by the rule table of the CMMC rule (`esp-rules`, data): a cloud service provider that handles CUI carries evidence of the FedRAMP level or the equivalence the rule requires; a provider that is not a cloud service provider is inside the scope (its assets are in scope in the roles of the kind) or holds its own certification; and a provider that holds none of those MUST be the finding `esp-not-covered`. The table MUST carry its source and the date it was read, and its wording beyond the part read (`definitions.md`) MUST be marked `uncatalogued`. Relies on audit rows: A3, C3.
- **FR-055**: For the kind `soc2-system`, a subservice organization MUST be an external system with its **method** (`carve-out` or `inclusive`): with `carve-out` its controls are not in the boundary and the complementary subservice organization controls the service organization relies on MUST be named, with `inclusive` its assets are in scope in the role `subservice-system`; and either way the existence of the subservice organization MUST be disclosed in the view (FR-073). Relies on audit rows: A3, C3.
- **FR-056**: For the kind `ephi-environment`, a business associate MUST be an external system with its `business-associate-agreement` (FR-043), and an asset of the fleet that creates, receives, maintains or transmits ePHI for a covered entity MUST be in scope in the role `ephi-system`, and a business associate of the fleet that handles a covered entity's ePHI MUST be the role `business-associate-system` of the boundary of that covered entity; a hybrid entity MUST be modelled as an `organizational` boundary (the legal entity) with an `ephi-environment` boundary as the part (FR-028) that names its health care components. Relies on audit rows: A3, C2.

## Posture of a boundary

- **FR-060**: The posture of a boundary MUST be a projection of the claim table (0043-asset-properties): for each member in a role, the properties the role requires (FR-061) and their readings, with the freshness and the coverage that 0043 defines, counted for the members of that boundary only, so that the same asset is judged for as many boundaries as it is in and the one reading serves them all. Relies on audit rows: B1, E2.
- **FR-061**: The **properties a role requires** MUST be data: for each kind and role, the properties of 0043 (encrypted at rest, reachable from the internet, logging on, multifactor authentication enforced, patched, owner known, and the rest of that table) and the requirement of the authority each serves (FR-008), so that a requirement is judged by readings and never by a rule in code; a role that brings a requirement for which no property exists MUST be shown as a gap of the model (`property-missing`), never as passing. Relies on audit rows: B1, B3.
- **FR-062**: The **health of a boundary** MUST be one typed document of: the coverage of dispositions (FR-027), the freshness and coverage of its readings (FR-060), the count of open findings by kind (those of this specification and of 0043 and 0044), the interconnections in force, overdue or unapproved (FR-042, FR-044), the inheritances in force or expired (FR-053) and the age of the last approval (FR-067); a boundary MUST NOT be shown as healthy by being declared. Relies on audit rows: E1, E2.
- **FR-063**: A change to a boundary MUST be classified as **significant** by a rule table (data, with its reasoning): the addition of an interconnection, of an external system, of an inheritance, of a data class or of an asset class; a change of categorization; the removal of an out-of-scope justification; and a change of an owner's role. This table is the reasoning of this change and not a transcription of any programme's policy on significant change (`definitions.md`, UNVERIFIED), and a programme's own rule MUST replace it where a kind carries one. Relies on audit rows: A3, E3.
- **FR-064**: A significant change that a person has approved MUST open the finding `reassessment-due` for the boundary, naming the change, until a person records the assessment that follows (an assessment result, an authorization decision, a report), and the finding MUST NOT be closed by the passage of time. Relies on audit rows: E3.
- **FR-065**: Every projection and every document of a boundary MUST be readable **as of an instant**: the boundary, its members, its dispositions, its interconnections and its inheritances at that instant are the fold of its journal up to it (FR-101), and the readings used are the ones the evidence branch holds up to it (0043-asset-properties FR-025); `boundaryAsOf(fleet, boundary, instant)` MUST be a pure function of the journal and the readings, so that an auditor can be shown what was true on a date without a second store. Relies on audit rows: E1, E4.
- **FR-066**: The difference between two versions of a boundary MUST be one typed `BoundaryDiff` of added and removed members, changed dispositions and roles, added and removed interconnections and inheritances, changed categorization and changed owners and constraints, with the laws that `apply(diff(a, b), a)` is `b`, that `diff(a, a)` is empty, that `diff(a, c)` is the composition of `diff(a, b)` and `diff(b, c)`, and that `invert(diff(a, b))` is `diff(b, a)`. Relies on audit rows: A1.
- **FR-067**: A change to a boundary MUST be a **plan**: the diff (FR-066), its significance (FR-063) and the hash of its canonical form; a person MUST approve it by that hash, as the owner of the boundary (FR-005); the approval MUST show the boundary, the diff, the dispositions that the change decides and the findings it opens or closes, MUST name the approver and the role, and MUST be a person's act in the local channel that an agent, a script and a pipe cannot make (`E-BOUNDARY-NOT-PERSON`). An agent MAY propose a change and read the proposal, and a proposal MUST change nothing. A change that is significant, or a boundary of a kind in which a regulator or an assessor relies on the approval, MUST NOT be approved by the person who proposed it (a second person approves) unless a person has accepted `roles-not-separate` for that boundary (FR-005). Relies on audit rows: A1, F1.
- **FR-068**: Where one principal holds roles that a kind says are to be held apart (the authorizing official and the system owner of a system, for one), the plan MUST say so, and the approval of that principal MUST be recorded with the finding `roles-not-separate` and its accepted reason and expiry; an accepted finding MUST be a person's act and MUST be shown in every view of the boundary. Relies on audit rows: A1, F1.

## Views

- **FR-070**: Every view (FR-071 to FR-077) MUST be a **projection**: a pure function from a boundary, the estate, the readings and an instant (FR-065) to a document, with no data of its own; a view that needs a fact the model does not hold MUST say `UNKNOWN` with the property and the role that needs it, never invent one (FR-079); and a view MUST name the boundary version, the instant and the evidence it used. Relies on audit rows: A1, G1.
- **FR-071**: The **system security plan** view of the kind `authorization` MUST be written as OSCAL by `grc` (the model `system-security-plan`): `system-characteristics` from the boundary's name, its categorization (the security objectives and the information types with their impacts) and its status; `authorization-boundary` from a description and the data-flow and boundary diagrams the SDK derives (FR-048), `network-architecture` and `data-flow` likewise; `system-implementation` with `components` (one per class of the members, with the roles and the responsible roles), `inventory-items` (one per member, naming the components it implements), `leveraged-authorizations` (the inheritances of the kind `leveraged-authorization`) and `users` (the roles of the people); and the interconnections as components of the type `interconnection` (FR-040). The free-text `boundary` of the assessment input of `grc` (`system.boundary`) MUST be replaced by this typed value, so that the OSCAL document is written from the model and not from a sentence. Relies on audit rows: G2, G3.
- **FR-072**: The **CMMC view** of the kind `cui-environment` MUST give, as the CMMC rule asks for each in-scope category: the inventory by category, the asset treatment (the role's treatment as data), the network diagram of the scope, and the security protection assets and the contractor risk managed assets and specialized assets with how each is managed; and the **register of out-of-scope assets** with each justification (FR-025). Relies on audit rows: A3, G2.
- **FR-073**: The **SOC 2 view** of the kind `soc2-system` MUST give the description a service organization's report holds: the services, the commitments and requirements (as data a person states), the components by the five groups of FR-024, the boundaries of the system (the members, the interconnections and what is out), the controls and their evidence, the subservice organizations with their method and the complementary subservice organization controls (FR-055), and the complementary user entity controls. Its field names MUST follow the Description Criteria once they have been read (`definitions.md`, UNVERIFIED) and MUST be marked `unread` until then. Relies on audit rows: A3, G2.
- **FR-074**: The **HIPAA view** of the kind `ephi-environment` MUST give the scope of a risk analysis: the systems that create, receive, maintain or transmit ePHI (FR-056) with where each does, the business associates and their agreements, the interconnections that carry ePHI, the health care components where the covered entity is a hybrid entity, and the readings that bear on the confidentiality, integrity and availability of that ePHI. Relies on audit rows: A3, G2.
- **FR-075**: The **record of processing** view of a boundary of the kind `privacy` MUST be written from the activities (FR-032) in the form its regime requires; for the GDPR and the UK GDPR the fields of Article 30(1): the name and contact details of the controller (and a joint controller, a representative and the data protection officer), the purposes, the categories of data subjects and of personal data, the categories of recipients, the transfers to a third country with the safeguard, the retention limits and a general description of the security measures; the processor's record of Article 30(2) MUST be a view of the boundary of the processor's own. A regime whose form has not been read MUST be marked `unread`. Relies on audit rows: A4, G2.
- **FR-076**: The **PCI view** of the kind `cardholder-data-environment` MUST give the CDE inventory, the connected-to and security-impacting systems, the segmentation (the crossings and their managed interfaces) with the evidence that the segmentation holds, and the systems out of scope with their justification, with the words of the PCI Security Standards Council's glossary and supplement (`definitions.md`). Relies on audit rows: A3, G2.
- **FR-077**: The **responsibility matrix** (FR-051) and the **plan of action and milestones** of a boundary MUST be views: the first from the inheritances, the second from the findings of the boundary through the POA&M of `grc` with their milestones (0043-asset-properties FR-017). Relies on audit rows: G2, G3.
- **FR-078**: A view MUST be written by a renderer that only draws or serializes it, and the same view MUST be what every surface prints (the command line, the tool server, the page, the report); where a renderer cannot draw a part, it MUST say which. Relies on audit rows: G1.
- **FR-079**: A view MUST show the **gaps** of its boundary beside its facts: the assets with no disposition, the roles with no required property in the model, the properties with no fresh reading, the interconnections with no agreement and the regimes whose text is `unread`. Relies on audit rows: E1.

## The smallest boundary

- **FR-080**: `suggestBoundary(estate, seeds, flows, rules)` MUST be a pure function giving the **closure** of a set of seed assets (those that hold a data class, such as every asset that processes CUI, ePHI or cardholder data) over the links and observed flows that the kind's rules say bring an asset into scope (an asset with unrestricted connectivity to one that holds the data, one that administers it, one that protects it), together with the assets the kind adds regardless (security functions). The closure MUST contain its seeds, MUST be idempotent (the closure of a closure is the closure), and MUST be monotone in the links (adding a link never removes an asset from it). Relies on audit rows: A1, D1.
- **FR-081**: A suggestion MUST give: the assets in the closure but not in the boundary (`missing`), the assets in the boundary but not in the closure and not in a security role (`excess`), and the links whose removal, or whose separation by a managed interface, would remove assets from the closure (`bridges`, with the assets each would free), so that an organization sees where a smaller enclave is possible and what it would take. Relies on audit rows: A1.
- **FR-082**: A suggestion MUST NOT change a boundary: it MUST be offered as a proposed plan (FR-067) that a person may approve, and an agent MAY produce and read it. Relies on audit rows: F1.
- **FR-083**: An asset that **joins the closure** of an approved boundary since the last approval (a new link, a new data class on an asset) MUST be the finding `scope-creep` that names the asset and the evidence, and MUST stay open until a person decides its disposition. Relies on audit rows: A1, E3.

## The interlock

- **FR-090**: `permits(action, target, boundaries, at)` MUST be a pure, total function giving `Permit` or `Refusal`, where the **actions** are one closed table that only grows of the active ones: `scan`, `pentest`, `apply` (a change of an asset's state), `install` (software put on an asset) and `enroll-active` (an enrollment that installs or changes), and where the passive ones (reading a realm's inventory, reading a log, observing a sighting) are not in it. Relies on audit rows: F2.
- **FR-091**: An active action on a target MUST be permitted only when the target has the disposition `InScope` in at least one **approved** boundary whose kind allows the action (`scan` and `pentest` by an `engagement-scope` boundary in its window; `apply`, `install` and `enroll-active` by an `organizational` boundary of the fleet that owns the target or by an `engagement-scope` that allows them), and when no approved boundary marks the target `excluded-target` or `OutOfScope` for that action: an exclusion MUST win over an inclusion, whatever the order, and a target with no disposition in any approved boundary MUST be refused. Relies on audit rows: F2.
- **FR-092**: A refusal MUST be one of `E-BOUNDARY-NOT-APPROVED` (the boundary's plan has no approval or its approval is of another hash), `E-BOUNDARY-NOT-IN-SCOPE` (the target is in none), `E-BOUNDARY-EXCLUDED` (an approved boundary excludes it) and `E-BOUNDARY-EXPIRED` (the window of the engagement is over or has not begun), and MUST name the target, the action, the boundaries consulted and the reason. A refusal MUST be written as evidence (an event of the audit chain) and MUST happen **before** any packet is sent or any change is made. Relies on audit rows: F2, E1.
- **FR-093**: The interlock MUST be in the SDK and not in a renderer: every library that performs an active action (the scanners, the penetration test runner, the appliers of Desired OS State, the installers of enrollment) MUST take the permit as a typed parameter, a value `Permit<Action>` made only by `permits`, so that a call with no permit does not type-check and a permit for one action does not serve another; a renderer MUST show the SDK's refusal and MUST NOT make its own. Relies on audit rows: F2.
- **FR-094**: A permit MUST name the boundaries and the version of each it relied on, its action, its target, its instant and its expiry (the earlier of the window of the engagement and a short interval), and MUST be recorded with the action's receipt, so that what was done can be shown to be what was allowed. Relies on audit rows: F2, E1.

## The documents

- **FR-100**: The documents of a fleet MUST be `boundaries/<id>.ts` (the boundary, typed code), `boundaries/<id>.journal.json` (FR-101), `boundaries/external-systems.ts` (FR-031) and `boundaries/agreements.ts` (references to the agreements, FR-043), all decoded on read (FR-004), and the evidence a boundary produces MUST be kept on the evidence branch under `evidence/boundaries/<id>/` with the readings' digests. Relies on audit rows: A2, E1.
- **FR-101**: The **journal** of a boundary MUST be an append-only, hash-chained record of its typed events `proposed`, `approved`, `refused`, `disposition-decided`, `override-made`, `interconnection-approved`, `interconnection-reviewed`, `inheritance-recorded`, `finding-accepted` and `retired`, each with its actor, role and instant, and the state of a boundary MUST be the fold of its journal (the same rule as the journal of a connection, 0044-asset-connections FR-005). Relies on audit rows: A2, E4.
- **FR-102**: The **plan hash** of a change MUST be the SHA-256 of the canonical form (the canonical JSON of the repository) of the boundary after the change, the diff and its significance, so that the same change always has the same hash and a changed byte has another. Relies on audit rows: A2, F1.

## Edge cases

- An asset in two boundaries of one kind with different dispositions (in scope in one, out of scope in the other): allowed, since the dispositions are per boundary; the overlap view (FR-010) shows it and nothing merges them.
- An asset that is `OutOfScope` and has a link to an in-scope asset with no managed interface: the finding `out-of-scope-contradicted` (FR-025), until a person records the separation or changes the disposition.
- A selector that takes in ten thousand assets at once (a realm's first discovery): the members are computed but every asset is `Undecided` until a rule proposes and a person approves; the proposal is one plan (FR-067) and not ten thousand approvals; `no-disposition` is one finding with a count and a list.
- A boundary whose owner's role is vacant: refused on read (FR-011); the last approved version stays in force and no new plan can be approved.
- An engagement whose window ends while a scan runs: the permit expires (FR-094) and the next step is refused (`E-BOUNDARY-EXPIRED`).
- A provider whose authorization lapses: the inheritance's evidence expires (FR-053) and the controls it covers show as the boundary's own, with no evidence, until it is renewed.
- A regime with `unread` text: its boundary can be drafted and read but not approved (FR-003).
- A real name in a boundary document of a public repository: refused by the secret and name checks of the fleet (FR-004).

## Assumptions

- The estate is the register of assets and links, and `grc` is where the claims of persons are signed; a boundary adds the declaration of accountability and does not replace either.
- A person is the authority for a boundary; the SDK proposes and checks and never decides what a regime requires of an organization.
- The categories and wording of a regime are data of the library, read from the regime's source and dated, and a counsel's reading governs where it differs from the table.

## Open questions

- **OQ-1**: Is a boundary of the kind `trust` declared or derived? Default: derived from the crossings of the other boundaries and from the declared processes and stores of a data-flow model, and declarable for a threat model that wants lines the estate does not have.
- **OQ-2**: Does a boundary of the kind `engagement-scope` live in the fleet that serves the client or in the provider's? Default: in the provider's fleet, with a reference from the client's, because the engagement is the provider's record (`tenancy`).
- **OQ-3**: How is the `location` of a cloud resource mapped to a jurisdiction? Default: by a table of the fleet that maps a provider's region to a country code, written by a person.
- **OQ-4**: Do the regimes other than the first three (the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and the CPRA) belong in the first version? Default: they are in the table, marked `unread`, and can be drafted but not approved until their text is read.

## Key entities

- **Boundary**: a typed, versioned, signed declaration of an accountability scope of a stated kind.
- **Kind**: the authority, roles, asset roles and agreements a regime defines.
- **Disposition**: the decision about one asset in one boundary: in scope in a role, inherited, external, out of scope with a justification, or undecided.
- **Selector**: a typed expression that selects assets, with the laws of a Boolean algebra of sets.
- **Interconnection**: an approved, agreed, reviewed crossing of a boundary.
- **External system**: a system the fleet relies on but does not manage, with what its party holds.
- **Inheritance**: what a provider's authorization or report supplies to a boundary.
- **Categorization**: the impact of each security objective as the high water mark of its information types.
- **Processing activity**: a purpose, basis, data, systems, recipients, transfers and retention of a privacy boundary.
- **Permit**: a value, made only by `permits`, without which an active action does not type-check.
- **Realm**: where assets are found (0044-asset-connections); not a boundary.

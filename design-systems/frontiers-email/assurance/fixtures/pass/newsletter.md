# Most pilots we reviewed never had a way to fail
Two of the five pilots we reviewed last year ran past their budget because nobody wrote down what failure looked like. Write that test before you start.

## Three signals end a pilot
- The metric misses its threshold
- The date passes with no signal
- The team can't name the next test

== Piano tuners in a city of a million

Using the factors from the reading, about how many piano tuners work in a city of a million people?

* [ ] About 2
** Too few: two tuners could manage only 2,000 tunings a year, a tenth of what the city needs.
* [x] About 20
** Right: 20,000 tunings a year, divided by 1,000 tunings per tuner.
* [ ] About 2,000
** Too many: 2,000 tuners could manage two million tunings, a hundred for every piano.

[#tunings-per-tuner,type=numeric,assesses=O1,answer=1000,tolerance=25%]
== Tunings one tuner can do

How many pianos can one tuner tune in a year, at four a day and 250 working days?

[.feedback]
Four pianos a day for 250 working days gives about 1,000 tunings a year.

== What a factor needs

Which of these belong on the line for each factor of an estimate?

* [x] The value you chose
** Yes: the value is what gets multiplied.
* [x] The reason for that value
** Yes: a reader who doubts the value needs the reason to argue with it.
* [ ] The source of every number you have ever read on the subject
** No: one reason per factor is enough to check it.

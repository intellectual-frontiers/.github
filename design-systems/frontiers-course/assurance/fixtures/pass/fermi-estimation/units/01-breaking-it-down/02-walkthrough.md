We are going to estimate how many piano tuners work in a city of a million people. Nobody publishes that number, so
we will build it from pieces we can reason about.

Start with households. A million people at about two and a half to a household gives us four hundred thousand
households. Now pianos. Most homes do not have one. Say one in twenty does, and we get twenty thousand pianos.

How often does a piano get tuned? Once a year is a fair guess for a piano someone plays. So the city needs about
twenty thousand tunings every year.

Now turn it around and look at one tuner. A tuner can do about four pianos a day, with travel between them. Work two
hundred and fifty days a year and that is a thousand tunings.

Twenty thousand tunings needed, a thousand per tuner. That gives us about twenty tuners in the city.

Is twenty right? We do not know yet. What we do know is every assumption that went into it, and each one is
written down where you can argue with it. If you think one home in ten has a piano, double the answer. That is the
whole method: factors you can defend, multiplied together, with the reasons in plain sight.

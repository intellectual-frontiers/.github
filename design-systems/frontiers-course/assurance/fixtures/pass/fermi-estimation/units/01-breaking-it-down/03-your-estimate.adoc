= Estimate the barbers in your city
:type: exercise
:minutes: 10
:outcomes: O1, O2

Estimate how many barbers work in your city. Write each factor on its own line with the reason for its value, then
multiply them out.

. How many people live in your city, and how many of them get their hair cut by a barber?
. How often does each of them go?
. How many haircuts does one barber give in a day, and how many days a year do they work?

Keep your factors. The next unit asks you to check your answer against a published count.

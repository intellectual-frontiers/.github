[#gap-of-ten,type=multiple-choice,assesses=O3]
== A gap of ten

Your estimate is 30 and a measured count is 300. What does the gap tell you?

* [ ] The estimate is close enough
** No: a factor of ten is the largest gap estimation should leave.
* [x] At least one factor was badly off
** Right: go back through each line and ask which assumption could move the answer that far.
* [ ] The measured count must be wrong
** Not on its own: check what the count includes before doubting it, and check your factors too.

[#gap-ratio,type=numeric,assesses=O3,answer=1.4,tolerance=0.1]
== The ratio of the gap

A board lists 28 tuners where you estimated 20. What is the measured value divided by the estimate?

[.feedback]
28 divided by 20 is 1.4, well within a factor of three.

[#method-name,type=text-match,assesses="O1, O2",answers="Fermi estimation, Fermi estimate, Fermi problem"]
== Name the method

What is this way of estimating usually called, after the physicist known for it?

[.feedback]
The method is named for the physicist Enrico Fermi, who was known for estimates of this kind.

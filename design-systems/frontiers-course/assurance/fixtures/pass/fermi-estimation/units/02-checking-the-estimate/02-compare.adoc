= Compare your barbers with a published count
:type: exercise
:minutes: 10
:outcomes: O3

Find a published count of barbers for your city or region: a licensing board, a census table or a trade association.
Compare it with your estimate from the last unit.

. Write the measured value, where it comes from, and what it leaves out.
. Work out the gap as a ratio.
. If the gap is more than a factor of three, name the factor you would revisit first and say why.

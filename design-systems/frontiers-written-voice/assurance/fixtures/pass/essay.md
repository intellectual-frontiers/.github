# We stop a project when the evidence says stop

A project ends when its numbers say it should, not when the team gets tired. I've watched good teams keep a dead product alive for a year because nobody wrote down what failure would look like.

Write that test before you start. Name the metric, the date and the threshold. If the metric misses the threshold on that date, the project stops, and the team moves to the next bet with what it learned.

We don't know yet whether a six-week window is long enough for every product. Two of our last five pilots needed ten weeks to show a signal, so the window is a default you can change, not a law.

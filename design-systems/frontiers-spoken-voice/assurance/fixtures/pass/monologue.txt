Most hospitals still buy software the way they bought it in 2005. A committee writes a list of features, three vendors answer it, and the cheapest one that checks every box wins.

That process picks the vendor with the best sales team. It doesn't pick the one whose product your nurses will use on a Tuesday night.

Picture a deal that dies late. The pilot went well, the contract was ready, and then the security review found that the product stores patient data in a region the hospital can't use. Nobody asked that question in month one.

So ask it in month one. Put the three questions that killed your last deal at the top of the next list.

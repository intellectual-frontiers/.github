The software of this repository is public to read and is licensed for noncommercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). A company, or anyone who uses the software commercially, needs a commercial license from Intellectual Frontiers LLC.
[`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md) says in plain words what is free and what is not.

## What a commercial license covers

A commercial license is an agreement between Intellectual Frontiers LLC and the licensee. It is meant to cover, as the agreement states:

- the libraries and programs of this repository the licensee names, at the versions it names or later, for use in the licensee's business;
- hosting, embedding, distributing and reselling the licensee needs, where the agreement says so;
- the term, the number of seats, sites or deployments, support and updates, where the agreement says so;
- what it does not cover: the trademarks (Mizan, Kanon, Archon, Stolos and the logos) unless the agreement grants a use of them, and the software of others listed in [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), which stays under its own license.

Terms differ by use: the owner may offer terms for managed service providers, for products that embed the software and for internal use in a company, and may decline a use.

## How to get one

1. Open an issue at <https://github.com/intellectual-frontiers/.github/issues> titled **Commercial license**.
2. Say who the licensee would be, what it wants to do with the software, which libraries it uses and how many people or machines are involved.
3. The owner replies in the issue with how to continue and with the terms offered. Nothing private belongs in the public issue.

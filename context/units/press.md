**The question Press asks:** What do we understand that is worth making

## Why Press exists

Press was not created just to publish books. It exists because every company
Intellectual Frontiers builds or funds should master three things: its
market, its craft, and its story. That company is expected to know the
customers, workflows, incentives, rules, alternatives, and distribution. It
is expected to build, deliver, support, measure, and improve what it
promises. It is also expected to explain the problem, its insight, its
Native Alpha, the evidence, and the next decision in its own words.

The firm does not wait for a journalist, analyst, public-relations firm,
trade publication, or conference to explain it correctly. Outside coverage
can bring reach, scrutiny, and independent validation. It should add to the
firm's voice, not replace it.

Books are one form. Press may produce research, field guides, case studies,
documentation, essays, diagrams, podcasts, briefings, newsletters, and direct
channels. What matters is whether the work improves understanding and helps
somebody make a better decision — not whether it fills a content calendar.

Studios companies receive the deepest help, because learning the market and
building a direct voice are part of building the company. Companies funded
by IF Capital may choose to work with Press. That help is collaborative and
optional: an investment does not give Press editorial control and does not
promise favorable coverage. The goal is capability transfer — the company
should learn to own its voice, source material, channels, and archive.

## Levels Press works at

| Level | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| Intellectual Frontiers | Why the firm exists, how Native Alpha governs decisions, and what the record supports |
| Business units | What IP, Press, Capital, Studios, and Network each do, and where their boundaries remain |
| Studios companies | Market thesis, customer evidence, category language, Native Alpha, and a company-owned voice |
| IF Capital companies | Optional help with research, launches, education, and direct channels without editorial control |
| IF Network | What the search found and what the evidence supports, written so a hiring decision can rest on it |

## The rule

Native Alpha is the advantage. Press makes it legible, credible, useful, and
durable. Press must never invent an advantage or use narrative to cover weak
evidence.

**Evidence Press produces:** useful thinking, trusted audiences, changed
behavior, and durable assets.

**Boundary:** a publication does not prove demand — see the constitution's
unit-boundary rule. See also [`context/brand.md`](../brand.md) for the voice
principles (evidence-led, practical, skeptical, plain) every piece Press
publishes should hold to.

# Intellectual Frontiers Network

> **Status: canonical.** Distilled from `doctrine.ts` (`network`),
> `corporate.ts` (`unitQuestions`), and `brand.ts` (`brandUnits`) at commit
> `6260a3b` of the Lovable-managed web repo (2026-09-13). Network is the
> fifth unit, added after the doctrine's v1.0 and after the brand book's
> v1.3 — it does not yet have an approved lockup of its own (see
> [`context/brand.md`](../brand.md)).

**Job:** Find and prove the people who can carry the work.

**Color:** Network Indigo `#4A4A8C`.

**The question Network asks:** Who has unusual knowledge, capabilities,
relationships, reputation, experience, or access relevant to an opportunity,
and how do we find them when needed without building another static resume
database?

## Why Network exists

Network is the newest unit and the one the original doctrine did not have. It
exists because the hardest constraint on the firm is rarely capital or
rights — it is finding the small number of people who can actually carry a
piece of work, and knowing enough about them to stake a decision on it.

The unit works the way the rest of the firm works. A search starts with a
written thesis: what the work is, what would have to be true about a person
to do it, and what evidence would change the firm's mind. Signals come from
public work, shipped systems, writing, code, filings, and the operating
record — not from a job title. What comes back is an evidence packet on a
small number of people, with what is known, what is inferred, and what is
missing stated separately.

Relationship memory is the compounding asset: three decades of operating
relationships in healthcare and government technology, held as a record of
who did what and how it turned out, so the next search starts further along
than the last one. Ask Network is how that record gets queried in plain
language.

Counts published about a search are illustrative unless the packet says
otherwise — and the packet says so in the same breath.

## The rule

An introduction is not a reference, and a reference is not evidence.
Network's output is an evidence packet that can change a hiring or
partnering decision, with the gaps named.

**Evidence Network produces:** search theses, signals, evidence packets,
relationship memory, and acted-on introductions.

**What Network must never become:** a rolodex. Its output is evidence about
people, not a list of names or a favor economy — see the constitution's
"what we will not become" section.

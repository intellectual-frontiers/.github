## Why this document exists

`context/units/capital.md` states Capital's charter in prose: where its
judgment comes from, what it owns versus hands to independent parties, and
the rule that no single signal obligates it to invest. This spec restates
the checkable parts of that charter as requirements. Where 0001 already
states a rule that covers Capital (FR-011, FR-012, FR-017), this spec
cites it rather than restating it.

This is not an implementation plan. It does not set fund structure, LP
terms, carry, or the underwriting model's actual formulas.

## Primary scenario

An opportunity — sourced internally through Studios, IP, Network, or
externally through a founder Capital has never worked with — is evaluated
against the same written underwriting standard, regardless of source.
Capital makes demand, distribution, governance, reserves, and return
assumptions explicit, tests whether the company has an unusual right to
win, and funds the smallest hard proof capable of changing the decision.
Nothing about a Studio's involvement, a patent, a publication, or a prior
relationship substitutes for that process. The final allocation decision
routes through Shahid N. Shah, with assumptions and dissent recorded, and
everything Capital is not built to do — administration, legal, tax, audit,
valuation, compliance — goes to independent parties.

### Acceptance scenarios

1. **Given** a Studios venture reaches paid proof, **when** Capital
   evaluates whether to fund it, **then** Capital applies the same
   underwriting standard it would apply to an external opportunity — no
   internal discount on the evidence bar.
2. **Given** a company presents a patent, a publication, a relationship,
   or a prototype as its case, **when** Capital evaluates it, **then**
   none of these alone obligates Capital to invest.
3. **Given** fund administration, tax, audit, valuation, or compliance
   work is needed, **when** it comes up, **then** Capital routes it to an
   independent party rather than performing it itself.
4. **Given** a capital-allocation decision is made, **when** it is
   finalized, **then** it routes through Shahid N. Shah as final
   authority, with the assumptions behind it and any dissent recorded.
5. **Given** a related-party or cross-vehicle decision involving Capital
   (funding a Studios company, working alongside Sinbad Capital or
   Netspective), **when** it happens, **then** a control exists that makes
   the decision independently reviewable — common ownership or trust
   between the parties does not substitute for that control.
6. **Given** Capital describes itself publicly, **when** it does, **then**
   it presents itself as a new formal investment platform built on a long
   operating, angel, board, and M&A record — not as an institutional fund
   manager with tenure it has not earned.

### Edge cases

- An opportunity sourced through Network or IP reaches Capital: the
  source unit does not change the underwriting standard applied to it.
- A specialist's licensed diligence opinion conflicts with Capital's own
  underwriting judgment: no doctrine states which one governs the final
  call. `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION: no stated resolution when specialist
  diligence and Capital's own judgment disagree]`
- Sinbad Capital or another independent partner is involved in a deal:
  Capital has no investment, allocation, or LP authority over them, and
  must not describe the relationship as if it did.

## Requirements

**Underwriting standard**

- **FR-001**: Capital MUST apply the same written underwriting standard to
  internally sourced (Studios, IP, Network) and externally sourced
  opportunities alike.
- **FR-002**: Capital MUST NOT allocate capital on the basis of a Studio's
  activity, a patent, a publication, or a relationship or prototype alone.
  (Restates 0001 FR-011 and `context/units/capital.md`'s rule.)
- **FR-003**: Capital MUST use first-principles underwriting to make
  demand, distribution, governance, reserves, and return assumptions
  explicit for every opportunity it evaluates.
- **FR-004**: Capital MUST apply the Native Alpha test — whether the
  company has an unusual right to win — as a check separate from, not a
  substitute for, the underwriting analysis.
- **FR-005**: Capital MUST fund the smallest hard proof capable of
  changing the investment decision, not the easiest visible activity.

**Division of labor**

- **FR-006**: Capital MUST route fund administration, legal, tax, audit,
  valuation, and compliance to independent parties rather than performing
  them itself. (Restates 0001 FR-012.)
- **FR-007**: Capital MUST route specialist diligence and licensed
  opinions to independent specialists rather than substituting its own
  judgment for licensed expertise.
- **FR-008**: Capital MUST name the assumptions behind every allocation
  decision and record any dissent, not just the outcome.

**Authority and identity**

- **FR-009**: Capital-allocation decisions MUST route through Shahid N.
  Shah as final authority. (Restates 0001 FR-017, Capital-specific.)
- **FR-010**: Capital MUST NOT present itself as having institutional
  fund-manager tenure it does not have. It MUST describe itself as a new
  formal platform built on a long operating, angel, board, and M&A record.
- **FR-011**: Any related-party or cross-vehicle decision involving
  Capital MUST have a control that makes it independently reviewable,
  regardless of common ownership or trust between the parties involved.
  (Restates 0001 FR-019, Capital-specific.)
- **FR-012**: Capital MUST NOT imply investment, allocation, conflict, or
  LP authority over an independent partner (for example, Sinbad Capital)
  that it does not actually hold.

**Fund-specific rules**

- **FR-013**: A company MUST contribute to a better end-to-end patient and
  caregiver experience to qualify for a care-delivery fund; this is a
  qualifying condition, not a preference to weigh against others.
- **FR-014**: Before a company is incorporated under a fund's "build"
  mode, its operating model, target buyer, contract chassis, and unit
  economics MUST be settled. If they cannot be settled, the build MUST
  NOT start.
- **FR-015**: A care-delivery fund MUST NOT operate as a traditional
  venture capital fund, private equity fund, accelerator, incubator,
  consulting firm, or short-term financial buyer, and MUST NOT force a
  common holding period, financing structure, or exit schedule onto every
  company it holds.
- **FR-016**: A fund's job MUST stay scoped to what it was built to prove.
  A fund proving that a set of reimagined pieces work together MUST NOT
  be treated as also responsible for carrying a company past that point —
  that is a different fund's job.

**Capital as a search function**

- **FR-017**: Before funding an internally originated venture, Capital
  MUST check whether an existing founder or company — sourced through IF
  Network or the broader market — already pursues the same thesis
  credibly. A thesis originating inside the firm MUST NOT, by itself,
  earn a pass on this check.
- **FR-018**: Where an external founder's evidence for the same thesis is
  as strong as or stronger than the firm's own, Capital MUST treat backing
  that founder as a live alternative to financing a competing internal
  build, not a fallback considered only after internal options are
  exhausted.
- **FR-019**: Capital MUST NOT measure itself by the amount of capital
  deployed; deployment volume is not evidence of good judgment.

**Capital sources**

- **FR-020**: Every dollar Capital deploys MUST be traceable to one of its
  two named sources — the firm's own balance sheet, or an LP or investor
  brought in through IF Network — or to an explicitly documented new
  source; a funding source MUST NOT go unnamed.

## Key entities

- **Underwriting judgment** — demand, distribution, governance, reserves,
  and return assumptions, made explicit for a given opportunity.
- **The Native Alpha test** — whether a company has an unusual right to
  win, applied alongside underwriting, not instead of it.
- **Mandate, reserves, governance, LP relationships** — what Capital owns
  directly.
- **Independent functions** — fund administration, legal, tax, audit,
  valuation, compliance, and specialist diligence, all held outside
  Capital itself.
- **Funds, GPs, and SPVs** — hold mandates and investor capital; no
  commingling with the parent or other vehicles.
- **Sinbad Capital** — an independent early fund and operating partner;
  Capital has no allocation or LP authority over it.
- **Care Delivery Fund I** — proves that a set of reimagined care-delivery
  pieces work together; not this spec's place to state its current size
  or roster (see `context/units/capital.md`).
- **Care Delivery Fund II** — in formation; backs clinician-led
  care-delivery companies through one of three modes (invest, take
  selective control, build).
- **Capital sources** — the firm's own balance sheet, and LPs and
  investors brought in through IF Network.

## Success criteria

- **SC-001**: An external opportunity and an internal Studios opportunity,
  given equivalent evidence, receive the same underwriting treatment —
  no observable internal discount.
- **SC-002**: No funding decision cites a patent, publication, Studio
  activity, or relationship as sufficient justification by itself.
- **SC-003**: Every allocation decision has a recorded assumption set and
  any dissent, not just a recorded outcome.
- **SC-004**: Fund administration, audit, tax, and valuation outputs come
  from parties independent of Capital's own underwriting team.
- **SC-005**: Capital's public description of itself never claims tenure
  beyond its actual formation date as a formal platform.
- **SC-006**: No care-delivery fund company is missing a settled operating
  model, target buyer, contract chassis, and unit economics at the point
  it is incorporated under "build" mode.
- **SC-007**: No care-delivery fund forces a uniform holding period,
  financing structure, or exit schedule across its portfolio.
- **SC-008**: For a thesis with a live external candidate, a documented
  comparison against backing that candidate exists before Capital funds a
  competing internal build.
- **SC-009**: Every dollar Capital deploys traces to a named source.

## Out of scope

- Fund structure, LP terms, carry, and fee mechanics are fund-formation
  decisions, not spec-level.
- The underwriting model's specific formulas and thresholds are
  operational detail.
- Sinbad Capital's or any other independent partner's own governance is
  outside Capital's authority entirely, and outside this spec.
- The funds' current size and portfolio roster are point-in-time business
  facts, not operating doctrine — deliberately left to the live site
  rather than restated here (see `context/units/capital.md`'s header
  note).

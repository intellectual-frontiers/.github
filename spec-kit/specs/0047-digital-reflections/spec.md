# Feature Specification: Digital reflections: Eidolons and Ergons

**Spec ID:** 0047-digital-reflections
**Status:** Draft

**Input:** How the Eidolon represents what a record is about. A digital
reflection is a maintained, AI-readable record of one subject. It is an
Eidolon when the subject exists independently of its record (a person, an
organization, a facility, an identifiable physical asset), an Ergon when
the subject was deliberately created to accomplish a purpose (a product, a
service, a system, a workflow, a solution), and a Noema when the subject is
an abstract intellectual construct (a concept, a hypothesis, a theory, a
methodology; 0048-noemas). This spec states the shared model, the three
kinds, the rules that classify a subject, the typed relationships that
connect them, the assertions that make a record evidence-backed and the
review that accepts them, the subjects that are none of the three, and how
records typed with the wrong kind are reviewed and moved without loss. The
terms live in the ontology (`ifcore:DigitalReflection`, `ifcore:Eidolon`,
`ifcore:Ergon`, `ifcore:Noema`), reusing W3C PROV, schema.org, Dublin Core
and SKOS under 0019-controlled-vocabulary.

## The shared model

- **FR-001**: A digital reflection MUST be a record of exactly one
  subject, typed as an `ifcore:Eidolon`, an `ifcore:Ergon` or an
  `ifcore:Noema`, all kinds of `ifcore:DigitalReflection`. The three kinds
  MUST be pairwise disjoint: no record is two of them, and none is a label
  for another.
- **FR-002**: A reflection MUST name its subject by a stable identifier
  (`ifcore:reflects`, one IRI). The subject is a different individual from
  the record about it. Reflections, relationships and assertions MUST
  refer to a subject by that identifier and MUST NOT match subjects by
  name.
- **FR-003**: A reflection MUST NOT be required to simulate its subject,
  stay synchronized with it, or be complete. What is not known MUST be
  stated as an assertion labelled `unknown` (FR-022), not omitted and not
  guessed.
- **FR-004**: A reflection is a `Fact` and MUST declare its audience
  (0001-eidolon-architecture FR-011), and MAY name the role that stewards
  the record by `ifcore:ownerRole`. Stewardship of a record MUST NOT be
  read as ownership of the subject, a legal right in it, or authority
  over it.
- **FR-005**: A reflection MUST support, using the terms the ontology
  already has, a stable identifier (`dcterms:identifier`, `schema:sameAs`
  for outside identifiers), the subject's types, assertions with their
  sources (FR-022 to FR-026), relationships (FR-016 to FR-021), the
  record's audience, and its history, which Git holds. A change to a
  subject MUST be a new assertion or a closed relationship (FR-020), not
  an overwrite that loses what was believed before.

## Eidolon

- **FR-006**: An Eidolon MUST reflect a subject that exists independently
  of its digital representation and is identified as an individual in the
  world: a person; an organization, which includes a company, a legal
  entity, a government agency and an institution; a place or facility; an
  identifiable physical asset or unit. An Eidolon's record is for
  answering what the subject is, what is known about it and how, what
  relationships it has, what has changed, what matters now, and what a
  person or an AI should understand or do next.

## Ergon

- **FR-007**: An Ergon MUST reflect a subject that was deliberately built,
  designed or assembled to accomplish a purpose: a software product or
  application, a commercial service, an API or platform, an engineered
  system, a business workflow, an AI agent or AI Workforce solution, a
  care delivery program, a manufactured or engineered product model,
  productized intellectual property. It stays one Ergon across its
  versions, which are related by `evolvesFrom` (FR-017).
- **FR-008**: An Ergon MUST state its purpose (`ifcore:purpose`) and SHOULD
  name whom it is meant to benefit (`schema:audience`). The outcome it
  promises MUST be related by `intends`; an outcome it has realized MUST be
  related by `produces` and supported by an assertion labelled `evidence`
  (FR-023). An Ergon's record is for answering why it was made, what
  problem it addresses, how it works, what it depends on and offers,
  who builds, owns, operates, buys and uses it, what it promises, what
  shows that it works, and how it should evolve.
- **FR-009**: An instance, a deployment or an execution of an Ergon MUST NOT
  be recorded as that Ergon, and is not automatically another Ergon. An
  individually identifiable unit of it is a subject of its own, related by
  `instantiates` (FR-017). An execution is an event (a `prov:Activity`)
  related by `executes`, and what it makes is a `prov:Entity` generated by
  it (0007-work-and-assets FR-001, FR-002).

## Classification

- **FR-010**: A subject MUST be classified by its identity (an individual
  in the world, or a kind, a design or a method), its purpose (deliberately
  made to accomplish something, or not), its abstraction level (an
  individual, a design of individuals, or an idea), and its lifecycle
  (exists before and apart from its record, or is designed and versioned).
  A subject MUST NOT be classified solely by whether it is physical,
  digital, human-made or naturally occurring.
- **FR-011**: The ontology MUST hold the classification as
  `ifcore:ClassificationRule` individuals, each naming one subject class
  (`ifcore:subjectClass`) and the one kind it reflects as
  (`ifcore:reflectedAs`): `eidolon`, `ergon`, `noema`, `other` (FR-027) or
  `undetermined`. At most one rule MUST name a class. A new subject class
  MUST receive its rule before reflections of it are created.
- **FR-012**: A subject's kind MUST be resolved from the rules of its
  types and their superclasses: the most specific matching rules decide.
  When they agree the subject has that kind. When they disagree, or when
  no rule matches, the kind is `undetermined`, and MUST NOT be defaulted
  to Eidolon or Ergon by any rule, tool or reader. A reflection MUST NOT
  be created for an `undetermined` subject until a person types it more
  specifically or adds a rule.
- **FR-013**: The initial rules MUST classify a person, a company or other
  legal entity, a government agency, a hospital organization, a place or
  physical facility, and an identifiable unit of a product as Eidolons;
  a product model, a software application, a software-as-a-service
  product, a commercial service offering, a designed workflow, an AI
  workforce solution or agent design, and an engineered care delivery
  program as Ergons; and a concept, a research finding, a research
  pillar, a named framework and an invention as Noemas.
- **FR-014**: A legal entity MUST be an Eidolon whatever brought it into
  existence. The defining distinction is its independently identifiable
  identity in the world, not whether people formed it.
- **FR-015**: An individually identifiable unit (a particular deployed
  medical device) and the model, design or software it was made from MUST
  be separate subjects with separate reflections, related by
  `instantiates`. An AI agent's design and operating contract is an Ergon;
  its running instances, identities and activity records MUST be modeled
  separately (FR-009). An organization and a product it makes MUST be
  separate reflections even when they share a name.

## Relationships

- **FR-016**: A relationship between two subjects MUST be an
  `ifcore:ReflectionRelationship` with exactly one type from
  `ifcore:ReflectionRelationScheme`, one `ifcore:relationFrom` and one
  `ifcore:relationTo`, each a subject identifier (FR-002). It is directional:
  swapping the endpoints states a different relationship.
- **FR-017**: The relationship types MUST include at least `worksFor`,
  `owns`, `inventorOf`, `assignsTo`, `licensesTo`, `develops`, `operates`,
  `offers`, `purchases`, `deploys`, `uses`, `serves`, `dependsOn`,
  `integratesWith`, `implements`, `intends`, `produces`, `instantiates`,
  `executes` and `evolvesFrom`. Each type MUST declare its family
  (`ifcore:relationFamily`: organizational, legal right, operational,
  commercial, technical, outcome or lineage) and the kinds its endpoints may
  have (`ifcore:fromKind`, `ifcore:toKind`). A relationship whose endpoint
  has a resolved kind (FR-012) that its type does not allow MUST fail the
  check; an endpoint whose kind is `undetermined` is not tested.
- **FR-018**: Legal ownership, inventorship, assignment, licensing,
  operation, development and use MUST remain distinct types, and none MUST
  be inferred from another. Relationship types MUST NOT be arranged in a
  hierarchy. The absence of one relationship says nothing about another.
- **FR-019**: A relationship MUST carry its provenance (`dcterms:source`,
  or `prov:wasAttributedTo` for who stated it), MAY carry its temporal
  validity (`schema:validFrom`, `schema:validThrough`), and MUST carry
  exactly one `ifcore:claimLabel` stating how settled it is. One labelled
  `hypothesis` or `inference` MUST also carry an `ifcore:confidenceLevel`.
  A relationship with no source MUST be labelled `hypothesis` or `unknown`.
- **FR-020**: A relationship that has ended MUST be kept and closed with
  `schema:validThrough`, not deleted. A relationship that changed MUST be a
  new relationship, so that what was true then can still be asked. A
  `schema:validThrough` MUST NOT precede its `schema:validFrom`.
- **FR-021**: A relationship MUST be stated between subjects, not
  between reflection records, so that moving a subject from one kind to the
  other (FR-032) never changes a relationship.

## Assertions

- **FR-022**: What a reflection holds about its subject, beyond its
  identifier, its types, an Ergon's purpose (FR-008) and its audience, MUST
  be an `ifcore:Assertion` about that subject (`schema:about`) with exactly one
  `ifcore:claimLabel` from the closed set of 0017-spoken-and-research-works
  FR-021 (observation, hypothesis, evidence, inference, recommendation,
  unknown). An observation, an inference drawn from it and a recommendation
  resting on both MUST be separate assertions.
- **FR-023**: An assertion labelled `observation` or `evidence` MUST name its
  source (`dcterms:source`), a `Reference` or other tracked fact, never a
  bare statement.
- **FR-024**: An assertion is verified when a person has checked it
  (`ifcore:verifiedBy`, a `Person`, with `ifcore:verifiedOn`). An assertion
  attributed to an AI agent (`prov:wasAttributedTo` a `prov:SoftwareAgent`)
  MUST NOT be verified by that agent, and MUST NOT be presented as verified
  unless a person's verification is recorded. A `hypothesis`,
  `inference`, `recommendation` or `unknown` MUST NOT itself carry
  verification: a person who confirms one records a new `observation` or
  `evidence`, derived from it by `prov:wasDerivedFrom`.
- **FR-025**: A `recommendation` MUST be derived (`prov:wasDerivedFrom`) from
  the assertions it rests on. Human judgment stays explicit: an AI
  inference is never established fact by being recorded.
- **FR-026**: Every assertion is subject to the sensitivity test
  (0001-eidolon-architecture FR-016). Personally identifying information
  about a person an Eidolon reflects, and a third party's confidential
  information about any subject, MUST be held only by a
  `RestrictedDataReference`.

## The boundary with other subjects

- **FR-027**: A subject that is an abstract intellectual construct (a
  scientific theory, a research hypothesis, a general concept, an abstract
  capability, a mathematical model, an invention) MUST NOT be given an
  Eidolon or an Ergon; its reflection is a Noema (0048-noemas). A subject
  that is none of the three kinds, such as an event, a legal right, a
  patent claim or other claim, a document or an outcome, has the kind
  `other` and no reflection.
- **FR-028**: A patent document, a legal right in it, the invention it
  describes and a commercial product that practices it MUST be distinct
  subjects, related by typed relationships (FR-016, FR-018): the invention
  is a Noema, the document and the right are of kind `other`, and the
  product is an Ergon. Who owns a right MUST NOT be inferred from who
  invented it, nor the reverse.
- **FR-029**: Retired. A third kind of reflection exists: FR-001 and
  0048-noemas govern it.
## Compatibility and migration

- **FR-030**: The IRI `ifcore:Eidolon` MUST stay valid, and a record
  already typed `ifcore:Eidolon` MUST stay served at its identifier,
  unchanged, until a person reviews it (FR-031). The meaning of the class
  is the one FR-006 gives; a record of a product, service or system made
  under the broader meaning is a candidate for review, not an error.
- **FR-031**: No tool, check or reader MUST reclassify a record on its own.
  A check MUST report a record whose subject resolves to the other kind,
  to `other` or to `undetermined` as needing review, naming the record and
  the resolved kind, and MUST NOT fail on it.
- **FR-032**: A person's reclassification of a record MUST create a new record
  of the right kind that reflects the same subject and is derived from
  the old one (`prov:wasDerivedFrom`, per 0007-work-and-assets FR-005); the old
  record MUST stay, pointing at the new one by `dcterms:isReplacedBy`. The
  subject's identifier and every relationship stay as they were (FR-021).
  A subject whose kind a person cannot decide keeps its record and is
  entered in the repository's register of open questions.
- **FR-033**: A change to this spec, the rules or the relationship types
  (a schema change) MUST NOT be made in the same commit as a change of a
  record's kind (a data migration). Each record's migration is its own
  reviewed change.
- **FR-034**: A reader that needs every reflection MUST ask for
  `ifcore:DigitalReflection`; one that needs only people and organizations
  asks for `ifcore:Eidolon`. A reader that treated every Eidolon as
  possibly a product MUST also read `ifcore:Ergon`, until no record is
  reported under FR-031.

## Naming and checking

- **FR-035**: The canonical names MUST be `Eidolon`, `Ergon` and `Noema`,
  with the plurals `Eidolons`, `Ergons` and `Noemas`. The Eidolon of
  0001-eidolon-architecture (the three-repository system, and a company's
  own, per 0011-studios FR-017) is the system that holds reflections; prose
  MUST NOT call a product, a service, a system or a workflow an Eidolon,
  MUST call a deliberately created subject an Ergon, and MUST call an
  abstract intellectual construct a Noema.
- **FR-036**: `agora check ontology` MUST fail on a classification rule that
  is malformed or duplicated, a reflection without exactly one subject,
  without an audience or typed as more than one kind, an Ergon without a purpose,
  a relationship or assertion that breaks FR-016 to FR-025, a replaced
  record that breaks FR-032, a required relationship type that is missing, and a relationship type that is
  arranged in a hierarchy (FR-018). It MUST also check the worked examples
  beside this spec.

- **FR-037**: Each kind has a represented subject, and the record MUST NOT
  be taken for it. The represented subject of an Eidolon is an
  independently identifiable real-world entity; of an Ergon, a deliberately
  created operational solution; of a Noema, an abstract intellectual
  construct. It is never the record itself, a document, page or database
  row that describes it, a claim or an assertion made about it, or another
  kind's idea of it. A subject does not change kind because its record
  exists as a document, a row or a software object: a Noema kept in a file
  is not an Ergon, and an Eidolon of a person is not the person's profile
  page.
- **FR-038**: An assertion MUST carry a review state
  (`ifcore:reviewState`): `candidate`, `reviewed`, `accepted` or
  `rejected`; none stated is `candidate`. An assertion attributed only to an
  AI agent MUST be `candidate` unless a person has reviewed it
  (`ifcore:reviewedBy`, a `Person`, with `ifcore:reviewedOn`), and a
  `reviewed`, `accepted` or `rejected` one MUST name its reviewer. Review
  accepts a statement into what the Eidolon holds; verification (FR-024)
  checks it against its source. A hypothesis may be accepted as a
  hypothesis and is never verified. A rejected assertion stays.
## Out of scope

- How a reflection is stored, indexed, searched or retrieved by a
  product; this spec fixes the identifiers and types a retrieval
  convention keys on, not an index.
- How a live record is kept current by a feed or a sensor.
- The migration of a particular repository's records; that is run, record
  by record, in that repository under FR-031 to FR-033.

## Edge cases

- A hospital described by one `schema:Hospital`, which is both an
  organization and a building: the kind is `undetermined` until the
  organization and the facility are separate subjects, per FR-012.
- A company formed by a founder: it is an Eidolon, per FR-014.
- A deployed device and its product model share a name: two subjects, two
  reflections, related by `instantiates`, per FR-015.
- A workflow run on Tuesday: an event related by `executes`, not an Ergon,
  per FR-009.
- A subject typed as both a company and a software application, because
  one name was used for both: the rules disagree, so its kind is
  `undetermined` until it is split into two subjects, per FR-012 and
  FR-015.
- A person invented a patented method and the company owns the patent:
  `inventorOf` and `owns` are two relationships; neither follows from the
  other, per FR-018 and FR-028.
- A relationship that ended last year: it stays, closed by
  `schema:validThrough`, and a new one records what holds now, per
  FR-020.
- An AI agent infers that a customer is evaluating a competitor: an
  `inference` attributed to the agent, not verified until a person
  records it, per FR-024.
- A product moves from Eidolon to Ergon in the register: a new Ergon
  derives from the old record, which is kept and points to it, per FR-032.
- An invention, a theory or a mathematical model: a Noema, per FR-027; a
  patent claim, an event or a legal right: kind `other`, no reflection, per
  FR-027.
- An idea described in a document, a database row or a software object: it
  stays a Noema, and the document is not its Ergon, per FR-037.

## Assumptions

- A subject's types are declared in the ontology of the repository that
  holds its reflection, so its kind can be resolved there.
- Schema.org, W3C PROV, Dublin Core and SKOS stay published and stable
  enough to reference by IRI, as 0019-controlled-vocabulary assumes.
- A person can decide a kind that the rules leave `undetermined` faster
  than a tool could guess it correctly.

## Open questions

- **OQ-2**: The kind of a running instance of an AI agent. It is an
  independently identifiable individual with a lifecycle, which points to an
  Eidolon by FR-010, yet it exists only as software and is created by
  instantiating a design, which points away from one. No rule classifies it,
  so it is `undetermined`.
- **OQ-3**: Whether the Eidolon of 0001-eidolon-architecture, the system, is
  renamed now that `Eidolon` also names one kind of record. FR-035 keeps both
  uses distinct in prose; a rename is a decision for the authority in
  effect.
- **OQ-4**: Whether the outcome of an Ergon (what it intends and
  produces) is a subject with a record of its own or only a measured
  assertion; the relationships treat it as kind `other`.
- **OQ-6**: Whether the class IRIs `ifcore:Eidolon` and `ifcore:NativeAlpha`,
  which also stand for the founder's research ideas, are split into a class
  of records and a separate Noema subject for the idea. Today one IRI plays
  both parts, and FR-037 keeps the two senses apart in prose.
- **OQ-5**: How the subject classes the rules leave `undetermined` are
  decided: a unit of the company (`ifcore:Unit`), an entry in the system
  inventory (`ifcore:InformationSystem`), and a digital asset
  (`ifcore:DigitalAsset`). Each mixes a design with a deployment, or an
  organization with a constructed role; the rules say so and decide nothing.

## Key entities

- **A digital reflection** (`ifcore:DigitalReflection`) - a maintained,
  AI-readable record of one subject, with identity, context,
  relationships, evidence, provenance, history and lifecycle. It is not
  necessarily a digital twin.
- **An Eidolon** (`ifcore:Eidolon`) - a digital reflection of what exists:
  a subject that exists independently of the record.
- **An Ergon** (`ifcore:Ergon`) - a digital reflection of what is created:
  a subject deliberately made to accomplish a purpose.
- **A Noema** (`ifcore:Noema`) - a digital reflection of what is
  conceived: an abstract intellectual construct (0048-noemas).
- **A represented subject** - the thing a reflection is a record of,
  named by `ifcore:reflects`; never the record.
- **A claim** (`schema:Claim`) - a statement that something is the case.
  It is not a reflection kind: a Noema states claims, and a record
  asserts things about its subject (0048-noemas).
- **A classification rule** (`ifcore:ClassificationRule`) - one subject class
  and the kind it reflects as.
- **A reflection relationship** (`ifcore:ReflectionRelationship`) - a typed,
  directional, sourced, time-bounded statement between two subjects.
- **An assertion** (`ifcore:Assertion`) - one labelled, sourced statement
  about a subject, verified only by a person.

## Success criteria

- **SC-001**: A company classifies as an Eidolon, a software-as-a-service
  product as an Ergon, a hypothesis as a Noema, and a subject with no rule
  or conflicting rules is never given a kind.
- **SC-002**: A device unit and its product model, and a workflow and its
  executions, are separate records and events, related by typed
  relationships.
- **SC-003**: A patent's ownership is never derivable from its
  inventorship, and a past relationship can still be asked about.
- **SC-004**: An assertion shows whether it is a sourced observation, an AI
  hypothesis or a person-verified fact.
- **SC-005**: A record made under the broader earlier meaning of Eidolon is
  still served, is reported for review, and moves only by a person's
  reviewed change that keeps its identifier and relationships.

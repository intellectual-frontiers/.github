# Vocabulary drift audit: 2026-10-09 (first run)

Rule: `0019-controlled-vocabulary` FR-010. Procedure: `spec-kit/audits/vocabulary-drift.md`.
Scope: the ontologies of `intellectual-frontiers/.github` (including the platform vocabularies and the Opsfolio platform) and `intellectual-frontiers/eidolon` (including `physia.ttl`). Nothing was renamed, removed or edited (FR-008).

## Summary

The scan read 782 terms: 43 reused (tied to an established term), 108 excepted (a Decision names them), 631 unmapped.
Unmapped means "not shown to be reused". It does not mean "invented". By kind, unmapped terms are 115 classes, 141 properties, 45 schemes and 330 concepts.
Of the 115 unmapped classes, 31 are named below as already using a standard word (Person, Organization, Trademark), so they only lack a mapping line; 9 are candidates to replace or rename; 12 are the company's own concepts that need a Decision. The other classes (mostly the ifweb page kinds and the design-system and press record classes) were read but not yet judged.

Method and limits: I read the definition of each class and each concept scheme in the platform and press vocabularies. I searched the web for 8 candidates, listed under "Sources". Verdicts marked *not verified* come from reading the definition only. The properties and the 330 concepts were not judged one by one; the scan lists them and the next run should sample them. This first run is the baseline.

## Candidates to replace or rename

| Term | Established term | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ifcore:Audience` ("a named permission to see a fact") | schema.org `Audience` already means "the group an item is intended for". ODRL has no `Audience`; its closest idea is a party and a constraint on a permission. | The two meanings clash, and the ontology's own comments already have to tell them apart (`ifcore:Readership`). | **Rename** to an access word (for example `AccessAudience` or a Permission-style term) or record a Decision. Needs a person. |
| `ifcore:ConsequentialSoftware`, `ConsequenceDomain` | FIPS 199 "potential impact" (low, moderate, high), which rates harm to operations, assets and people. | FIPS 199 secondary copies. The press definition (cost falls on other people than the decider) is wider than FIPS 199, so a full replacement does not fit. | **Map** to the impact idea and keep the press meaning only if a Decision says why it differs. |
| `ifcore:Hunt`, `SearchStage` | "Search" (the recruiting and sourcing word). `Hunt` already extends `prov:Activity`. | *Not verified.* | **Replace** the label with "Search". |
| `ifcore:NetworkMeasure` | "metric" or "indicator". | *Not verified.* | **Replace**. |
| `ifcore:RelationshipMemory` | "interaction history" (the CRM word). | *Not verified.* | **Replace**. |
| `ifcore:EvidencePacket` | `prov:Bundle` is "a named set of provenance descriptions". | PROV-O. An evidence packet holds cited signals, not provenance descriptions, so `prov:Bundle` fits only partly. | **Keep, map** to `prov:Collection` or `prov:Entity` with a note, or replace by "dossier". Needs a person. |
| `ifcore:IndependenceTest` | "key-person" or "continuity" test (business continuity). In audit, "independence" means something else. | *Not verified.* | **Rename**; `independence` clashes with the audit meaning. |
| Platform capabilities `Programmatic interface`, `Governed access`, `Authoritative catalog`, `Integration seam`, `Ontology model` (0049-platforms) | API, access control, system of record or data catalog, integration layer, semantic model. | Judged from the definitions. *Not verified* against an external source. | **Replace the labels** or record that each differs. Each label is a concept of `PlatformCapabilityScheme`. |
| `ifcore:LaborAsCode`, `ifcore:AIWorkforce` | "skill" and "agentic workflow" are the established words for what the definitions describe. | Judged from the definitions. | **Replace**, unless a Decision shows it is the company's own research. |

## Candidates to map (the term is a standard concept under a local name)

These classes use a standard word and only need one mapping line each (FR-009): `ifcore:Person`, `Organization`, `Company`, `Asset`, `TangibleAsset`, `IntangibleAsset`, `FinancialAsset`, `RealProperty`, `Equipment`, `Goodwill`, `IntellectualProperty`, `Copyright`, `Trademark`, `PatentFamily`, `License`, `Right`, `Fund`, `Agreement`, `Disposition`, `ControlTest`, `SharedService`, `ConfidenceLevel`, `Decision` (its parent `prov:Entity` is too general to count), `Alternative`, `Grant`, `Outcome`, `Capability`, `Invention`, `Note`, `Reference`, `Fact`.
Proposed targets: `schema:Person`, `schema:Organization`, `schema:Corporation`, `schema:Trademark` (or the legal term), `dcterms:RightsStatement`, `dcterms:License`, `prov:Entity` plus a specific parent, and the ISO 55000 and GAAP/IFRS asset classes that 0007-work-and-assets already cites.
`ifcore:AIFirst` and `ifcore:AIAugmented` use words that practitioners use (AI-assisted, AI-augmented, AI-native, AI-first) but no standard defines them, and sources disagree on the levels. Keep, and record that fact in a Decision (see below).

## Exceptions that need a Decision

A Decision names a term by `dcterms:subject` and lists the standard terms it considered. These terms have none:

- `ifcore:NativeAlpha`, `ifcore:AcquiredAlpha`. The founder's own research; the user confirmed the exception. Add the Decision. `Acquired Alpha` is the mirror of Native Alpha and should be named in the same Decision.
- `ifcore:IPLG` (Intellectual Product-Led Growth). "Product-led growth" is an established term (popularized around 2016, per one vendor source that the next run should check against its originator). The new part is "Intellectual" and the one-action-before-an-ask test. Keep the name and have the Decision say so.
- `ifcore:EvidenceLadderStage` (Silence, Argument, Adoption of terminology, Willingness to pay) and `ifcore:ClaimLabel` (observation, hypothesis, evidence, inference, recommendation, unknown). The Oxford CEBM levels rank study designs and GRADE rates certainty of an effect estimate; neither describes a publication's reception or a claim's role. Keep, with a Decision that cites both.
- Elenchus and Operational Truth. The established name is *continuous controls monitoring*: automated, repeated tests of whether controls operate, with evidence collected as a by-product. The company's method does the same and adds the comparison of intent held as code with evidence. The spec 0045 already uses auditor words for the parts. Needs a Decision that says why "continuous controls monitoring" does not name the whole method, or a rename.
- The named-tool classes in `ifpriv:` (`Debt`, `Engine`, `Floor`, `Ladder`, `Map`, `Rule`, `Test`) and `ifpriv:BareMetalSoftware`, `ifpriv:SoftwareSovereignty`, `ifpriv:ChiefOfStaff`. Plain words used as type names; the first group is a typology of the company's tools. Needs one Decision for the typology and a check of the other three against standard terms.

## Exceptions to re-check

- `ifcore:Eidolon`, `Ergon`, `Noema`: exceptions are accepted. Their Decisions (`EidolonErgonSeparation`, `NoemaThirdKind`) list alternatives but not **digital twin**. ISO 23247 defines a digital twin framework for manufacturing and names personnel, equipment, processes, facilities and products as the things twinned. An Eidolon (a reflection of what exists) is close to a digital twin of a person, company or facility. Add the alternative and the reason it is rejected, or the reason it is kept.
- `Noema` is also an established word in philosophy (Husserl). The Decision should say it is borrowed, not invented.

## Platforms (Opsfolio, Physia)

- `physia.ttl` defines no vocabulary. It holds individuals that use `ifcore:Platform`. No finding.
- The Opsfolio platform's modules (Evidence Store, Ontology Model, Integration Engine, Assurance Environment, Commitment Ledger, Control Catalog) are `ifcore:PlatformModule` individuals named after the kernel capabilities. Their names inherit the findings on the capability labels above. Product names (surveilr, Fleetfolio, Qualityfolio, Spry) are brand names of individuals, not vocabulary; out of scope.
- `ifcore:Platform`, `Suite`, `PlatformModule` and `ifcore:OfferKindScheme` (platform service, solution, product, service) use ordinary words; they lack mapping lines only.

## Not verified

No external source was checked for: `Hunt`, `NetworkMeasure`, `RelationshipMemory`, `IndependenceTest`, the platform capability labels, `LaborAsCode`, `AIWorkforce`, and the originator of "product-led growth". The ifpriv terms were read but not searched. The 141 properties and 330 concepts were not judged.

## Second pass: every remaining term judged

On the founder's instruction (accept every change that clarifies a term or ties it to an existing standard; make best judgements on the rest; history does not matter because the repository is new), the pass judged the remaining 141 properties, 330 concepts and the classes and schemes not yet judged. The result is in `ontology/ifcore.ttl`, `ontology/ifweb.ttl` and the vault's `ontology/works.ttl`. The scan after the pass reads 785 terms: 90 tied to an established term, 695 excepted by a Decision, 0 unmapped.

Changes that tie or clarify (all accepted by `ClarifiedAndMappedTermsAccepted`):

- **Class renamed:** `ifcore:Audience` is now `ifcore:AccessAudience` (Decision `AccessAudienceName`). The vault's code that looked the class up (`eid/lib/rdf.py`, `eid/lib/insight/index.py`) uses the new name. The property `ifcore:hasAudience` keeps its name.
- **Properties tied to a standard:** `legalName` to `schema:legalName`; `displayName` to `schema:name`; `doi` to `schema:identifier` (and a close match to `bibo:doi`); `decidedBy` to `prov:wasAttributedTo`; `decidedAt` to `prov:generatedAtTime`; `validFrom` and `validUntil` to `schema:validFrom` and `schema:validThrough`; `primarySource` to `prov:hadPrimarySource`; `describes` to `schema:about`; `grantedTo` to `odrl:assignee`; `unitOf` and `hasUnit` to `org:unitOf` and `org:hasUnit`; `sourceFile` to `dcterms:source`; `relationFrom` and `relationTo` to `rdf:subject` and `rdf:object`; `seriesNumber` to `schema:position`; `builtBy` to `dcterms:creator`.
- **Classes tied:** `Hunt` to `schema:SearchAction` (a search is the established word); `NetworkMeasure` close to `qb:MeasureProperty`; `EvidencePacket` close to `prov:Bundle`; the twelve content-page kinds of `ifweb` to `schema:WebPage`, the portfolio index to `schema:CollectionPage`, the promotion post to `schema:Article`, the application to `schema:WebApplication`.
- **Established names added as alternative labels:** the nine platform capabilities (for example "API" for the programmatic interface, "access control" for governed access, "data catalog; system of record" for the authoritative catalog) and "Interaction history" for relationship memory.
- **IPLG:** now "Intellectual product-led growth", a subclass of the new `ifcore:ProductLedGrowth` (the established term, with no published address found), stated as a subset and an elaboration of it.

Kept (each named by a Decision with alternatives): the evidence ladder, the claim labels, Elenchus, AI Workforce, Labor as Code and Consequential Software as the company's own framework terms; AI First and AI Augmented as practitioner terms; the Trust Services Criteria, the CMMC Level 1 practices and the risk treatment options as the published standards' own names; and the remaining classes, properties and schemes as ordinary words of their field. The earlier proposals to replace `IndependenceTest`, `NetworkMeasure`, `Hunt` and `RelationshipMemory` by other words are withdrawn: the first is an ordinary word whose supposed clash with auditor independence does not hold, and the others now carry a tie or an alternative label.

Limits of this pass: the 141 properties and 330 concepts were judged from their definitions and from knowledge of PROV-O, schema.org, DCMI Terms, the W3C Organization Ontology, ODRL, SKOS and the Data Cube vocabulary, and were not searched one by one. The ties for `bibo:doi` and `qb:MeasureProperty` and `schema:SearchAction`, `schema:validFrom`, `schema:validThrough` and `schema:position` were checked on the web or in documentation read in this session; the others (`schema:legalName`, `schema:name`, `schema:about`, `schema:identifier`, `schema:WebPage`, `schema:CollectionPage`, `schema:Article`, `schema:WebApplication`, `prov:wasAttributedTo`, `prov:generatedAtTime`, `prov:hadPrimarySource`, `odrl:assignee`, `org:unitOf`, `org:hasUnit`, `dcterms:source`, `dcterms:creator`, `rdf:subject`, `rdf:object`) are from knowledge of the vocabularies and were not searched.

== Brand assets and design systems

`design-systems/frontiers-brand/` holds the palette, the unit colors, the typefaces, the logo (light and dark), the icon, the app
icons, the unit marks, the imagery pool, the share card and the decoration kit. It themes every other design system. The others each
have a directory, listed with their kinds in <<reference-design-systems>>: the web, print, figures, slides, media, email, courses,
merchandise and the house voice. Every public house rule about how Intellectual Frontiers looks, reads or sounds belongs in a design
system of its kind here too (spec 0014).

Every design system carries an `assurance/` harness that runs on its own, by the means its README documents. `agora check
design-systems` runs them all, and CI runs `agora check --suite browser`, `--suite python` and `--suite images` on every push that
touches `design-systems/`.

* *Logos* are in `design-systems/frontiers-brand/logos/`, as PNG, with WebP in `logos/web/`. Use the `-dark-` variants on dark
backgrounds.
* *Pictures* are the brand's imagery pool, `design-systems/frontiers-brand/imagery/`. The app icons and the share card are in
`design-systems/frontiers-brand/images/`. Figures are drawn with `design-systems/frontiers-figures/`; the profile's are written by `agora
figure generate`.
* Consumers *vendor a pinned copy* and never edit it downstream. Change a design system only by amending its own source (spec 0014).
* Reference assets by path rather than copying them, so there is one source of truth. In `profile/README.md` the images use absolute
`raw.githubusercontent.com` URLs on `main`, because GitHub does not resolve relative paths on the organization profile page.

This guide follows its own rule. Its logo and pictures are the brand's, copied from `design-systems/frontiers-brand/` into the built
site by `agora docs build`, and nothing is drawn for it.

The help topics `design-systems` and `brands` say how to check a design system, start one, and regenerate a brand's files.
